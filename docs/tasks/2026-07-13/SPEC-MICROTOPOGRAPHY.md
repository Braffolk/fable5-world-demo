# SPEC: Cooked Decimeter Microtopography

**Project:** `laas`, an Estonia 1:1 streamed open world

**Date:** 2026-07-13

**Status:** Replacement implementation specification; Stage 1/diagnostic Stage 2 implementation is in progress, while unsupported morphology families and structural terrain remain gated by this contract

**Decision:** Build a deterministic, beauty-first, covariate-conditioned hybrid in `asset-gen`: absolute height rungs at physical LODs `-2` and `-1` for single-valued ground, plus cook-produced structural terrain clusters for steep, vertical, undercut, or overhanging outcrops; perform no terrain synthesis in the browser
**Target:** Believable sub-meter geometry grounded by parent material, soil, hydrology, land use, and real measured 2.5D/3D exemplars, represented by the geometry type the landform actually requires

---

## 1. Purpose and outcome

This document replaces every earlier microtopography specification and is the sole implementation contract for this task. An implementer is expected to start from the clean repository baseline identified in Section 4, not from the current uncommitted experiments.

The shipped result shall make ground-level terrain visibly richer and more credible. It shall not merely pass an upsample/downsample identity test. A user walking through the world must see morphology that changes for defensible reasons: peat differs from mineral soil, exposed carbonate differs from deep sediment, tilled ground differs from unmanaged grassland, wet hollows differ from drained ground, and forest disturbance differs from agricultural structure.

The result is synthesized rather than reconstructed. Maa-amet's 1 m DTM does not uniquely determine sub-meter relief. Orthophotos, soil polygons, and geological maps improve conditional plausibility but do not turn invented decimeter geometry into survey truth. Product copy, metadata, and code comments must call the result **synthesized microtopography**, never recovered or 1:1 sub-meter terrain.

### 1.1 Required user-visible outcome

At fixed ground-level review sites and camera paths:

- The `0.0625 m` height surface must visibly affect silhouettes, contact shadows, normals, foot placement, and near-ground parallax on single-valued ground; structural clusters must do the same on non-heightfield outcrops.
- Spatial character must vary continuously with soil, surficial geology, drainage, land use, and locally observed structure.
- The surface must not read as generic noise, repeating stamps, 128 m checkerboards, one-meter quilting, or arbitrary biome changes.
- Walking, camera rotation, and sun motion must not reveal shimmer, crawling normals, cracks, apron lines, LOD pops, or fine-coverage holes.
- The renderer must actually retain and draw the packed fine geometry. A correct file that is simplified away by the terrain DAG is a failed result.

### 1.2 Non-goals

- Do not reconstruct unknown sub-meter Estonia with false metric claims.
- Do not generate rocks, roots, litter, grass blades, or material-only bump as a substitute for terrain geometry.
- Do not replace the existing soil, debris, vegetation, or terrain material projects.
- Do not produce a national `LOD-2` bake in the first release.
- Do not add a browser procedural displacement, fBm, learned decoder, parameter field, or runtime micro-height generator.
- Do not adopt PyTorch, diffusion inference, or a GPU training pipeline for version 1.

## 2. Non-negotiable laws

1. **Zero browser synthesis.** Every microtopography height is generated by `asset-gen` and stored in the release. Runtime code may fetch, decode, cache, interpolate, downselect, and sample stored heights. It may not invent height.
2. **Final packed geometry, not parameters.** Single-valued ground is packed as absolute EH2000 height, not procedural parameters. Non-heightfield outcrops are packed as final cooked structural terrain clusters. Both are immutable release artifacts; neither is reconstructed or synthesized in the browser.
3. **128 m physical fine chunks.** The finest height chunks cover `128 m x 128 m` and contain `2049 x 2049` samples at `0.0625 m`. This is the correct fine-bin size.
4. **Do not redefine the shared content grid.** `grid.chunk_m = 2048`, height LOD0 is `1 m`, and existing LOD numbers for trees, biome, soil, water, canopy, understory, debris, boulders, and the generated source remain unchanged.
5. **Beauty is the objective; hierarchy consistency is a constraint.** LOD reconstruction, quantization, determinism, and seams are hard gates. They are not evidence of realistic morphology.
6. **Factorized grounding.** No single dominant class selects a generic roughness preset. Parent material, sediment thickness, soil, moisture, terrain, land use, disturbance, and observation confidence remain separate conditioning factors until morphology selection.
7. **No decorative noise.** White noise, fBm, chunk-local wrapped noise, coordinate-hash biome assignment, and a global roughness multiplier are forbidden as shipped geometry.
8. **One canonical finest field.** Generate LOD `-2` once in world coordinates. Derive LOD `-1` from that exact result. Never synthesize the two rungs independently.
9. **Chunk boundaries are not feature boundaries.** Random ownership and feature placement depend on absolute EPSG:3301 coordinates and a versioned recipe seed, never on chunk-local coordinates, worker assignment, cook order, or LOD.
10. **No partial publication.** An interrupted, stale, mixed-grid, or incomplete cook must be structurally impossible to publish as `latest`.
11. **Visible verification is mandatory.** Fixed-pose images and fixed-path ground-level motion captures are release gates. Statistical reports cannot waive visible defects.
12. **Representation follows topology.** A finer heightfield may not be used to fake a vertical, undercut, cave, or overhanging surface. Such regions require the structural-cluster path or remain explicitly unsupported and excluded from acceptance claims.
13. **No second geometric material surface.** Format-2 packed terrain compiles out `terrainDispAt` and any equivalent runtime vertex/root displacement. Procedural material variation may affect shading only; it may not move terrain, grass roots, collision, or contact geometry.

### 2.1 Explicitly rejected architectures

- **Runtime procedural micro-displacement:** rejected because it violates the zero-browser-synthesis law.
- **Global `chunk_m = 128` and old height LOD0 moved to LOD2:** rejected because `WorldGrid` is shared by all layers and the generated source; it spatially corrupts existing content.
- **Whole-chunk hashed landform classes plus Gaussian noise:** rejected as ungrounded and visibly discontinuous.
- **Diffusion-precompute for version 1:** rejected because no suitable paired decimeter Estonia corpus, trained model, deterministic CPU path, throughput budget, or demonstrated advantage exists. It remains a later challenger under Section 18.
- **Bare sparse OMP as the whole generator:** rejected because ambiguous low-frequency patches repeat atoms and do not model event morphology or causal spatial variation.
- **Heightfield-only Taevaskoda cliff:** rejected because a single-valued `H(E,N)` cannot represent the reference wall's vertical and undercut surfaces; increasing sample density only produces a steeper ramp.

## 3. Premise audit: go up one level

The following instruction is required verbatim and governs this design:

> When a problem resists solving, or a result disappoints, do NOT start varying your approach to the problem — that stays inside the problem. Go up one level first, to the context the problem lives in: the surrounding system, the upstream decisions, the goal that made this a problem, and everything you've been treating as the fixed environment it sits inside. What you filed under 'given' is the prime suspect, precisely because you filed it under 'given' and never looked at it. Put the background on trial: What generates this problem? Is that context flawed in a way that PRODUCES this failure? Can I feasibly change the context instead of out-thinking a problem that shouldn't have existed? Only if the context is genuinely fixed, or genuinely sound, drop back down and solve the problem where it sits.

### 3.1 What was incorrectly treated as given

The rejected work treated these as givens:

- Fine terrain had to become globally named `LOD0`.
- Downsampling identity was the central research problem.
- A land-cover label was enough to choose a terrain style.
- Realism could be obtained by normalizing a random field to a published amplitude.
- A fine height plane would automatically make the Nanite terrain mesh fine.
- Existing work directories could safely represent the current recipe.

Every item is false in this repository.

### 3.2 The real objective and fixed context

The objective is believable visible geometry near the viewer. The user has also fixed the storage-versus-runtime decision: all detail is cooked and packed. That leaves room to change the following surrounding systems:

- Height may have finer physical LODs while non-height layers retain their established grid meanings.
- Terrain-field and DAG lattice math may be normalized to the finest stored height rung.
- Release building may become recipe-addressed and transactional instead of globbing a shared directory.
- The generator may use the rich soil taxonomy already present rather than inventing a chunk class.
- Geological and orthophoto inputs may be added because realistic variance cannot come from the DTM alone.

### 3.3 Consequence of exact LOD0 preservation

The authoritative Maa-amet 1 m DTM constrains the mean surface in every 1 m cell. This prevents the generator from freely inventing new multi-meter hummocks or depressions whose cell means would change. Large microforms already expressed by the DTM may be smoothly reconstructed and sharpened; new residual synthesis is concentrated below the 1 m support.

This is an important beauty constraint, not a reason to weaken consistency silently. A process model claiming to add a new 2-3 m hummock while also preserving every 1 m block mean is physically misleading: the projection would have to add compensating depressions and distort the form. Version 1 therefore:

- reconstructs the measured 1 m macro surface with a smooth conservative interpolant;
- uses DTM evidence for forms at and above 1 m;
- synthesizes the genuinely underdetermined residual below 1 m;
- evaluates whether the exact 1 m constraint causes visible one-meter patterning;
- does not advertise unsupported larger invented forms.

If later art direction requires newly invented 1-4 m forms, that is a separate decision to regenerate the 1 m parent while preserving a coarser authority. It is not smuggled into this task.

### 3.4 Consequence of single-valued height

The literal `0.0625 m` heightfield is not the complete visible-terrain objective. A heightfield stores one elevation for each horizontal coordinate and therefore cannot represent vertical faces, undercuts, caves, or overhangs. The supplied Suur Taevaskoda reference contains near-vertical sandstone faces and undercut ledges at river level; no interpolation, residual generator, qscale, or finer rung can recover that topology. Treating the heightfield as fixed background would reproduce the original category error at a higher resolution.

Version 1 therefore separates terrain by representation:

- walkable and otherwise single-valued ground uses the absolute `-2/-1` height hierarchy;
- steep exposed-rock confidence selects a transition band in which height residuals are suppressed and a cook-produced structural terrain mesh owns the visible face;
- vertical, undercut, cave, and overhanging geometry comes only from a lithology-specific 3D exemplar/process family backed by TLS or oblique photogrammetry, packed as final renderer clusters;
- the structural mesh stitches into the unchanged height authority along a conservative single-valued collar and owns matching collision, material coordinates, shadowing, and vegetation exclusion there;
- absent a valid 3D source family, the region remains base terrain and is marked unsupported. It may not receive invented cliff noise or pass cliff-face acceptance.

This extension obeys the zero-browser-synthesis law: runtime work is limited to demand, decoding, culling, drawing, collision sampling, and selecting between already cooked representations.

`asset-gen/src/assetgen/manifest.py` collects every `.lac` present in the shared work directory and publishes the resulting index. It does not check:

- expected AOI key sets;
- complete LOD coverage;
- child-parent closure;
- grid or recipe identity;
- stale files from previous cooks;
- source or generator provenance;
- country-floor presence;
- whether verification ran.

`height_cook.py` treats an existing chunk as current when its `qscale` matches. Generator code, source inputs, AOI, masks, grid, exemplar data, and recipe revision are ignored. Other layer cooks use similar existence-based skips. The experimental release therefore mixed old-grid and new-grid chunks and published an interrupted cook.

### 4.4 Why the experimental generator looks wrong

The current untracked `micro_synth.py`:

- assigns one of bog, alvar, till, field, forest, or default to an entire chunk using a coordinate hash;
- does not consume the existing soil, geology, hydrology, or orthophoto data;
- does not use its `base_1m` argument;
- creates a Gaussian-filtered random field with `mode="wrap"` inside each chunk;
- places process primitives in chunk-local coordinates;
- independently seeds LOD0 and LOD1;
- globally normalizes mixed processes to one RMS target;
- zeros east and south apron detail rather than copying the neighbor's canonical samples;
- synthesizes over water and no-data after NaNs become sea level;
- allocates full `2049^2` arrays repeatedly per primitive and is not viable at pilot scale.

These choices guarantee arbitrary geology, chunk seams, cross-LOD disagreement, repetition, and implausible amplitudes. They are explicitly forbidden by this specification.

- `GeneratedWorldSource` is rechunked to 128 m despite retaining a 1 m native heightfield.
- The grass ray path adds per-blade sampling before correctness or performance is demonstrated.
- `PlaneFill` changes an offset to `stride/2` while also adding `0.5`, which misplaces sample centers.
- The separate negative-LOD worktree changes signed wire fields without a format bump and tries to reshape an apron-bearing `2049 x 2049` array into blocks whose dimensions do not divide 2049.

None of these changes carries forward automatically.

## 5. Frozen architecture and packed terrain extensions

### 5.1 Frozen global grid; proposed signed height rungs

Source files: `asset-gen/config/base.toml`, `asset-gen/src/assetgen/grid.py`, `src/world/source/WorldSource.ts`.

The global grid remains:

```toml
[grid]
anchor_e = 368640
anchor_n = 6635520
chunk_m = 2048
lod_step = 4
lods = [0, 1, 2, 3, 4]
chunk_res = 2048
```

For a height LOD exponent `l`:

```text
texel(l)     = 1 m * 4^l
footprint(l) = 2048 m * 4^l
res          = 2049 samples including the east/south apron
```

LODs `0..4` are the verified current height architecture. LODs `-2/-1` in the table below are the proposed format-2 extension; they do not exist in the clean format-1 base release.

Therefore:

| Height LOD | Texel | Chunk footprint | Purpose |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `-2` | `0.0625 m` | `128 m` | canonical micro surface |
| `-1` | `0.25 m` | `512 m` | near parent rung |
| `0` | `1 m` | `2,048 m` | authoritative Maa-amet DTM |
| `1` | `4 m` | `8,192 m` | established mid/far rung |
| `2` | `16 m` | `32,768 m` | established far rung |
| `3` | `64 m` | `131,072 m` | country floor |
| `4` | `256 m` | `524,288 m` | country floor |

The 128 m bin the user wants is exactly physical LOD `-2`. It does not require a `32769^2` payload or a global grid rewrite.

### 5.2 Exact sample-center contract

For any height chunk `(l, cx, cz)`, sample `(i, j)`, `0 <= i,j <= 2048`:

```text
t = 4^l meters
F = 2048 * t meters
gameX = originX + cx*F + (i + 0.5)*t
gameZ = originZ + cz*F + (j + 0.5)*t
```

In EPSG:3301:

```text
E = anchorE + cx*F + (i + 0.5)*t
N = anchorN - cz*F - (j + 0.5)*t
```

No `stride >> 1`, `stride / 2 + 0.5`, or implicit coarse-sample offset may replace these equations. Every cook and runtime placement test shall use these equations as its oracle.

The apron sample at `i=2048` is the same canonical world sample as the east neighbor's `i=0`; `j=2048` is the south neighbor's `j=0`. The float64 master values must be equal before quantization. Because decimal qscales and qoffsets are represented in binary floating point, decoded values are compared with the explicit tolerance in Section 11.3, not for bit identity.

### 5.3 Existing height encoding

Source files: `asset-gen/src/assetgen/cook/height_cook.py`, `asset-gen/src/assetgen/cook/encode.py`, `asset-gen/src/assetgen/cook/chunkio.py`.

- Height is quantized to u16 with per-chunk `qoffset` and `qscale`.
- The u16 raster receives a two-dimensional wrapping delta transform.
- Existing LAC1 and new LAC2 payloads use deflate in version 1; the current browser worker has no zstd decoder.
- Cook decodes its own result and checks the quantization error.
- A raw `2049^2` u16 payload is `8,396,802` bytes.
- A decoded `2049^2` f32 payload is `16,793,604` bytes.

The round-trip check remains, but version 1 must also add seam, hierarchy, coverage, and visible-output gates.

### 5.4 Existing renderer path

The height layer is assembled into camera-window `TerrainField` planes by `PlaneFill` and `StreamBrainCore`. Terrain tiles are baked through `TerrainStreamer` and `BuildHeightGrid` into the Nanite-style DAG. CPU `heightAt` and GPU field samplers also use these planes.

This path currently assumes a 1 m terrain base:

- `layerGeom(height).texel0` is 1 m;
- `StreamBrainClient` sends tile `cell = 1 m`;
- `StreamBrainCore` fixes `baseStride = 1`;
- clipmap levels double stride from that base.

Adding negative files alone does not make fine terrain. Section 13 defines the required normalization.

## 6. Research audit and architectural decision

### 6.1 What the cited literature actually establishes

**Guérin et al. 2016, Sparse representation of terrains for procedural modeling.** The method learns paired low/high terrain dictionaries, finds sparse coefficients from a low-resolution patch with OMP, reconstructs from the corresponding high-resolution atoms, and blends overlapping primitives. It is a legitimate amplification precedent. The [paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/cgf.12821) and [official MATLAB reference](https://github.com/eric-guerin/terrain-amplification) do not provide a ready NumPy package or Estonia-specific decimeter atoms. Deterministic behavior requires fixed patch order, dictionary, solver, tie-breaking, and arithmetic.

**Argudo, Chica, and Andujar 2018.** The correct orthophoto-guided terrain paper is [Terrain Super-resolution through Aerial Imagery and Fully Convolutional Networks](https://doi.org/10.1111/cgf.13345), not the Visual Computer citation used in `r2`. It supports registered aerial imagery as useful conditioning when paired high-resolution DEM training data exists. It does not support converting arbitrary RGB edges into height.

**Lochner et al. 2023.** [Interactive Authoring of Terrain using Diffusion Models](https://doi.org/10.1111/cgf.14941) provides real evidence that diffusion can improve perceived macro-terrain realism over a cGAN in its authored `256 x 256` setting. Training used PyTorch/diffusers and an A100. It does not validate Estonian decimeter morphology or a NumPy/SciPy cook.

**InfiniteDiffusion / Terrain Diffusion.** The [official repository](https://github.com/xandergos/terrain-diffusion) for the SIGGRAPH 2026 work (DOI `10.1145/3799902.3811080`) exposes models around 30 m and 90 m per pixel. Order-independent overlapping synthesis ideas are relevant; the learned prior is not evidence for `0.0625 m` Estonia.

**DDNM.** [DDNM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.00490) establishes range/null-space data consistency for a specified linear inverse problem. It does not generate geology, soil structure, or beauty. The required projection can be implemented directly without adopting diffusion.

**MultiDiffusion.** [MultiDiffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08113) fuses overlapping latent-image diffusion paths. It does not guarantee height, slope, normal, or apron continuity in this renderer.

**Blau and Michaeli 2018.** The [perception-distortion paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Blau_The_Perception-Distortion_Tradeoff_CVPR_2018_paper.html) establishes a frontier for restoration estimators. It neither forbids L2 diagnostics nor proves diffusion is the correct terrain generator.

**Process morphology.** [Doane et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1029/2024AV001264) models topographic roughness as event shapes plus decay and gives an implementable first-derivative-of-Gaussian pit-mound couplet. [HOHUM](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-3800(03)00067-X) grounds peat hummock/hollow structure in biological-hydrological feedback. These support explicit events where causal knowledge is strong.

**Estonian soils and microtopography data.** [EstSoil-EH](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/13/83/2021/) and the repository's Mullastikukaart parser provide soil type, texture, coarse fragments, stoniness, peat distinctions, and fertility. A public [SPRUCE TLS dataset](https://mnspruce.ornl.gov/datasets/spruce-microtopography-of-experimental-plots-derived-from-terrestrial-laser-scans) provides 1 cm peatland surfaces. It is an analogue, not Estonia truth.

### 6.2 Data-resolution correction

AHN4's public DTM is `0.5 m`. It can contribute at wavelengths above `1 m`, preferably comfortably above that Nyquist limit, but cannot calibrate the `0.5-1 m` band or provide ground truth for wavelengths represented only by a `0.0625 m` lattice. National Nordic or Estonian airborne LiDAR has the same limitation unless a specifically dense patch is proven otherwise.

The decimeter band requires TLS, close-range photogrammetry/SfM, or equivalent dense point clouds. Material scans and Megascans may calibrate only the finest surface band and only when their physical scale and substrate provenance are known; they are not landscape microtopography exemplars.

### 6.3 Decisive recommendation

Version 1 shall be a **deterministic exemplar plus structural-process hybrid**:

1. Build continuous factorized covariate fields from existing DTM, soil, land cover, water and mapped features, plus new surficial-geology data.
2. Reconstruct a smooth conservative fine base from the 1 m DTM.
3. Transfer measured, detrended high-frequency residual patches from class-restricted exemplar pools with deterministic covariate/overlap scoring and a single measured-energy-preserving quilting window.
4. Add explicit compact event morphology only where causal masks support it.
5. Project the combined residual into the zero-mean-per-1 m constraint with the AOI-wide minimum-energy projection in Section 9.6.
6. Quantize LOD `-2`, derive LOD `-1` from the decoded canonical child surface, verify, and pack.
7. Where 3D outcrop evidence proves that `H(E,N)` is invalid, cook lithology-specific structural terrain meshes, stitch them to the heightfield collar, cluster them offline, and pack them as a separately demanded terrain layer.

This architecture ships in NumPy/SciPy, exploits the repository's actual data, and makes a trained neural stack unnecessary. Diffusion is retained only as an experimental challenger with the same packed output contract.

## 7. Input data and confidence model

### 7.1 Existing mandatory inputs

All inputs are read in EPSG:3301 and sampled in absolute world coordinates.

| Input | Existing source | Native/use resolution | Role |
|---|---|---:|---|
| DTM | Maa-amet mosaic via `process/mosaic.py` | `1 m` primary | project-defined authoritative 1 m block target, slope, curvature |
| Soil type | Mullastikukaart `soilType` | polygon, cooked `2 m` | soil group, peat/gley/carbonate distinctions |
| Fine-earth texture | `texCore` | cooked `2 m` | clod/aggregate and drainage priors |
| Coarse fragments | `texSkeleton` | cooked `2 m` | stony residual and exposure likelihood |
| Stoniness | `stoniness` | cooked `2 m` | event density/style prior |
| Fertility | `boniteet` | cooked `2 m` | ecological/disturbance prior, not height amplitude alone |
| Land cover | ETAK `classId` | cooked `2 m` | forest, agriculture, wetland, bare, urban, water masks |
| Vegetation cover | `vegDensity`, canopy, tree records | `2 m` plus records | forest/open confidence, disturbance context |
| Water | ETAK water/watercover | `2 m` | hard no-synthesis mask and wet-edge context |
| Roads/buildings | ETAK geometry | vector | hard or feathered suppression masks |

The generator must read these source products directly or through a recipe-pinned covariate cache. It must not infer a whole chunk's class from a few samples.

### 7.2 New mandatory production input: surficial geology

Add `asset-gen/src/assetgen/fetch/geology.py` and `process/geology.py`.

The fetcher shall snapshot:

- Quaternary deposit composition;
- Quaternary thickness where available;
- geomorphology;
- bedrock lithology;
- bedrock relief or depth proxy where available;
- source map scale and coverage confidence.

Use WFS, not rendered WMS pixels, for vector conditioning. The primary endpoints are EGT's `https://maps.egt.ee/geoserver/k50/ows` and fallback `https://maps.egt.ee/geoserver/k200/ows`; the legacy public service `https://teenus.maaamet.ee/ows/wms-geoloogia` may be used only when its WFS capabilities expose the required vector feature type. The `50 m` WCS at `https://teenus.maaamet.ee/ows/wcs-geoloogia` is confidence/context only and must never position individual microforms. These endpoints and their purpose are documented by the [EGT service catalog](https://gis.egt.ee/geoportaal/teenused) and [Maa- ja Ruumiamet service catalog](https://geoportaal.maaamet.ee/eng/services/public-wms-wfs-p346.html).

### 7.5 Matrix selection and structural events are separate

The selector emits two independent products per 1 m cell. `matrix_weights` is a
simplex over mutually substitutable measured surface matrices:

```text
mineral_forest, bare_mineral, aggregate_clod, peat_surface,
wet_mineral, agriculture_oriented, carbonate_exposed, sandstone_exposed,
zero_detail
```

`event_factors` is a non-normalized record containing eligibility, confidence and
fitted rate for each additive structural process:

```text
forest_pit_mound, wheel_track, headland, fracture_karren
```

An event never takes probability mass away from its underlying matrix. For example,
a forest pit-mound modifies a selected mineral-forest matrix; it does not compete
with that matrix in a sum-to-one vector.

Each production bank manifest must declare a frozen selector model with mandatory
input channels, normalization, missing-data behavior and fitted coefficients. The
minimum mandatory matrix channels are `parent_material`, `cover_thickness`,
`soil_group`, `soil_texture`, `coarse_fragment_class`, `wetness`, `land_cover`,
`slope`, and `hard_mask`. An optional channel may not receive a nonzero coefficient.
Fit continuous coefficients and categorical likelihood tables on calibration sites
only by leave-one-site-out maximum likelihood; freeze the fitted values and training
site hashes in the bank manifest. A bank is ineligible when fewer than 70% of its
declared weighted channels are known. Source positional uncertainty is propagated
by area-weighting categorical likelihood across the uncertainty support, not by
snapping to the dominant polygon.

Hard eligibility conjunctions apply before scoring:

- `carbonate_exposed` requires carbonate parent material, thin-cover evidence, suitable soil/stoniness, and bare/open cover.
- `sandstone_exposed` requires matching bedrock lithology, thin or absent cover, exposed-ground evidence, and a valid 2.5D residual corpus; steep or non-single-valued exposure is routed to structural terrain clusters instead of the height residual.
- `peat_surface` requires peat soil or deposit evidence; wetness and mire type change the exemplar pool.
- `agriculture_oriented` requires agricultural land cover; orthophoto or parcel geometry supplies orientation.
- `forest_pit_mound` requires forest context and a fitted event model using tree population, slope, moisture, soil-strength proxy and disturbance evidence.
- `wet_mineral` requires gley/wetness evidence and is suppressed on mapped drained or engineered surfaces.

Unknown geology does not become default rock. Missing covariates reduce confidence.
If no eligible measured bank remains, `zero_detail=1`; the cook does not substitute
generic noise. The initial Lapinjarvi pilot is an explicit exception with one enabled
matrix, `mineral_forest`, only inside matching forest/mineral/mesic context; every
event and every other matrix family is disabled until a matching corpus and fitted
selector exist.

### 8.4 Residual extraction

Add `process/microtopo/exemplars.py`.

For each surface:

1. Reproject/resample onto the global `0.0625 m` sample-center convention.

These are rejection gates, not tuning targets; a failure requires diagnosing extraction or excluding the plot rather than loosening limits to make it pass.
6. Compute the authoritative 1 m cell means with the exact box reducer used by the cook.
7. Reconstruct the smooth conservative base defined in Section 9.2.
8. Store residual = measured surface - conservative base.
9. Store the raw detrended residual plus validity/confidence. Do not permanently multiply stored patches by Tukey or another edge window. The synthesizer applies exactly one declared partition window; double tapering is forbidden because it creates stride-phase amplitude modulation.
10. Record per-band PSD, semivariogram, anisotropy, slope, curvature, amplitude quantiles, feature density, and zero-crossing statistics.

### 9.2 Smooth conservative base

Nearest-neighbor expansion of the 1 m DTM is forbidden because it creates one-meter terraces.

Implement a tensor-product conservative cubic B-spline:

1. Treat the DTM value as the required area mean of each 1 m cell under this project's hierarchy contract. This is a deliberate renderer/cook authority definition, not a claim about the provider's pixel-estimator semantics; record the provider semantics separately if they can be verified.
2. Construct a uniform cubic B-spline basis over cell-edge knots for the complete aligned synthesis coverage rectangle, with at least the declared 32 m source halo outside published cores.
3. Build the one-dimensional banded matrix `K` whose entries are the average of each basis function evaluated at the 16 exact LOD `-2` sample centers inside a 1 m cell. Continuous spline integrals are not the authority: the discrete 16-sample operator must be the same operator used by `box_mean_16`.
4. Solve the separable system `Kz * C * Kx.T = Y` using deterministic float64 banded solves from `scipy.linalg.solve_banded`.
5. Store the AOI-wide coefficient field as a recipe-addressed float64 memmap and evaluate requested `256 m + halo` windows at `0.0625 m` sample centers into float64.

The solve is performed once over the complete coverage rectangle, not independently per supertile; separable row/column passes stream through memmaps. This is what makes base samples canonical at execution-window boundaries. It yields a C2-continuous fine base whose exact 16x16 cell means equal the source DTM within `1e-7 m` before quantization. Linear extrapolation in the external halo handles AOI edges; published cores never include the extrapolated rim.

The unconstrained cubic is accepted only when every fine value in a 1 m cell lies inside the min/max of that cell's valid 3x3 DTM neighborhood within `1e-6 m`, and its fine-grid slope does not exceed twice the largest valid centered source slope in that neighborhood plus `0.02`. On violation, reconstruct the affected connected region with tensor-product `scipy.interpolate.PchipInterpolator`, then enforce each source cell mean with the bubble `w(u,v)=[u^3(1-u)^3][v^3(1-v)^3]` and a deterministic bound-constrained active-set redistribution. PCHIP is C1, not C2; therefore any coverage containing fallback cells has an explicit C1 base contract. The bubble preserves value and first-derivative continuity but does not upgrade PCHIP to C2. Stable row-major clipping/tie-break is required. The fallback must pass the same mean, value, slope and C1 continuity gates plus the fixed rendered normal/motion review, or fail the cook. It may not silently clamp the final surface. Diagnostics record the exact fallback mask and fraction.

Required passing unit fixtures include planar, tilted-plane, quadratic-bowl, broadened smooth peak, and real-DTM patches. An isolated one-cell impulse is an expected deterministic rejection fixture: exact peak/neighbor means, a no-overshoot envelope, and bounded continuous slope are mathematically incompatible for that input. It must fail with `PATHOLOGICAL_DTM_EXTREMUM`, never clamp or emit a cliff. Gates:

- planar/tilted reconstruction error `< 1e-7 m`;
- max 1 m block-mean error `< 1e-7 m`;
- no value leaves its local 3x3 source envelope and no slope exceeds the bound above;
- no spectral spike at exactly 1 m above the same interpolant without constraints;
- no value or first-derivative discontinuity at internal 1 m boundaries above float tolerance.

### 9.3 Deterministic exemplar placement

The execution core is aligned to a `256 m x 256 m` global supertile containing `2 x 2` LOD `-2` chunks. Generate with a `32 m` halo on all sides. The halo exceeds the version-1 maximum exemplar support (`16 m`) and event support radius (`24 m`). Candidate/event ownership is global, so every execution window enumerates all owners whose declared support intersects its working extent; two windows evaluating the same sample therefore enumerate the same contributors. Components and accumulators that affect height are float64 and processed sequentially or through memmaps so peak resident memory stays below Section 16.

Candidate centers lie on a globally aligned overlap grid with bank-specific patch stride. Build one placement graph over the complete planned AOI plus halo; chunks and worker windows never own selection. For every center:

1. Compute local factor weights, DTM derivatives, orientation, and confidence.
2. Reject exemplar banks whose factor tags conflict with hard context.
3. Score low-resolution context with the exact normalized weighted distance in Section 8.4.
4. Retain the calibrated top-`k` candidate set using stable `(context_score, source_site, patch_id)` ordering. `k` is a bank parameter and must pass the diversity gates below; it is not fixed globally. The initial three-source Lapinjarvi diagnostic uses `k=12`, because measured placement audits show `k=8` over-concentrates patches and suppresses the highest-variance source.
5. Minimize a frozen AOI-wide labeling energy containing context error, left/right/up/down overlap error, and a bank-fitted source/site balance prior. Use deterministic row-major ICM sweeps from coordinate-hash initialization, exact float64 costs, stable patch-ID tie-break, and a fixed maximum sweep count/convergence threshold stored in the recipe. This is deterministic exemplar quilting, not Guérin paired-dictionary amplification; do not describe it as OMP or sparse reconstruction.
6. Select only a stored rotation variant allowed by the bank metadata. Agricultural and flow-oriented patches align to the nearest stored observed axial direction; geological fracture sets use only stored conjugate directions.
7. Store raw residual patches and apply exactly one synthesis partition. The default overlap uses complementary sine/cosine equal-power weights, accumulated as `sum(w_i*p_i)/sqrt(sum(w_i^2))`, so independent measured residual energy is not attenuated in overlap zones. A bank may instead use a deterministic minimum-error seam that copies measured samples without crossfade, but the seam algorithm and topology gates must be pinned. Convex `sum(w_i*p_i)/sum(w_i)` crossfade is forbidden unless held-out evidence proves no stride-phase amplitude loss.

All stochastic decisions use domain-separated SHA-256 counters with explicitly serialized little-endian integers. Poisson event counts use inverse-CDF traversal from a SHA-256 uniform; no NumPy global RNG or version-dependent distribution sampler is allowed. Contributors accumulate in stable `(center_n descending, center_e ascending, bank_id, patch_id, rotation_id)` order, independent of worker scheduling. The temperature is calibrated per bank and stored in bank metadata. There is no user-facing global diversity or roughness knob.

Repetition and quilting gates reject:

- the same patch ID occupying more than 5% of placements in a 512 m review site;
- normalized cross-correlation peaks above `0.85` at nonzero offsets for more than 1% of review windows;
- visible periodicity at placement stride in the PSD.
- placement-phase RMS or gradient-RMS variation outside the held-out exemplar envelope;
- generated RMS, amplitude tails, joint height/slope/curvature, or source contribution outside the calibrated bank envelope after quilting;
- an effective patch count below the bank minimum or a source-site mix outside its frozen confidence interval.

### 9.4 Structural event modules

Events are additions to measured residual transfer, not replacements for it. Every version-1 event has zero support outside a radius of `24 m` and is owned by a globally aligned event cell. Event count uses the Section 9.3 SHA-256 inverse-CDF Poisson draw keyed by absolute event-cell coordinate, module ID, and recipe version. Event metadata pins cell size, support, parameter CDF tables, and fitted source sites.

Implement these modules in `events.py`:

**Forest pit-mound couplet**

```text
zeta(x,y) = 2*A*x/lambda_x^2 * exp(-x^2/lambda_x^2 - y^2/lambda_y^2)
```

Use the Doane first-derivative-of-Gaussian form and draw orientation, age, and decay from calibrated distributions or direct treefall/wind evidence. Do not impose systematic downslope orientation without supporting observations. Event probability may depend on forest context, tree density/age proxy, slope, wetness, and soil-strength proxy only when that dependency is fitted to exemplar/event data; otherwise disable the event. Do not place an event merely because land cover says forest.

**Agricultural oriented structure**

Represent furrow cross-sections with measured exemplar profiles modulated along the row. Orientation comes from high-confidence orthophoto structure tensor or parcel/headland geometry. Add separate sparse wheel-track/headland events. Clod residual comes from the matching tillage-state exemplar bank. Soil texture affects the conditional bank only after management state.

**Peat surface**

Use measured peat residual patches as the version 1 source. A separate peat event is disabled until its shape and parameter distribution are fitted to valid exemplars. Do not synthesize new multi-meter hummocks or string/flark structures that the exact 1 m authority would erase; version 1 may only transfer sub-meter residuals and refine patterns already expressed by LOD0.

**Exposed carbonate**

Use measured, resolvable thin-soil/carbonate residual patches. A fracture or karren event requires high `carbonate_exposure` and a shape/orientation distribution supplied by exemplar metadata or mapped/observed local evidence. Unresolved features below the corpus and lattice sampling limit are omitted. Soil-covered limestone receives no visible grike embossing.

**Exposed sandstone and other bedrock**

Use lithology-specific families; never reuse carbonate fracture/karren morphology for sandstone. A single-valued exposed sandstone bench may use a measured 2.5D residual bank. A steep, vertical, undercut, cave, or overhanging exposure is ineligible for height residual synthesis and is routed to Section 9.8. Stratification, ledges, recesses, fracture spacing, weathering profiles, and block failure require a 3D corpus and fitted joint distributions; a 2D geology polygon only selects/confidences a family and never supplies those shapes.

**Mineral fallback**

Use conservative measured mineral-soil residuals conditioned by texture, coarse fragments, stoniness, moisture, and land use. There is no synthetic generic-noise fallback. If no compatible measured bank exists, residual is zero and confidence is low.

### 9.5 Masks and transitions

- Open water: residual exactly zero; base is the conservative fine reconstruction of the carved/bed LOD0 policy produced by the established water pass.
- Buildings and paved roads: residual zero inside the footprint; feather only outside the mapped edge over `0.25-0.5 m`.
- Unpaved tracks: use a mapped/profile event only when classification supports it.
- DTM no-data/sea: no micro chunks are published unless valid-ground coverage satisfies the AOI policy.
- Morphology transitions: blend residual contributions by continuous factor weights before projection. Never select one class for a whole chunk.

### 9.6 Smooth hierarchy projection

Let `A` be the exact 16x16 area reducer from LOD `-2` to LOD0. Let `R` be the combined exemplar plus event residual. A blockwise constant subtraction is forbidden because it can print a one-meter grid.

First set `R=0` at every fine hard-mask or open-water sample. Do not correct each 1 m cell with an identical cell-centered bubble: repeating one basis at every cell structurally creates the one-meter phase pattern the gate is meant to reject.

For fully soft connected terrain, compute `e=A(R)` over the complete AOI and reconstruct a globally C2 correction `C` with the same conservative tensor-product B-spline machinery as Section 9.2, using the exact discrete 16x16 mean operator. Set `R0=R-C`. For a haloed connected band touching hard samples, solve the sparse constrained problem

```text
minimize ||C||^2 + lambda_g ||gradient(C)||^2 + lambda_k ||laplacian(C)||^2
subject to A(R-C)=0 and C=0 on hard samples
```

with fixed float64 CSR ordering, solver, tolerances, and regularization values learned on calibration sites and stored in the bank recipe. If fewer than 25% of a cell's samples are soft, zero that cell residual before assembling the solve. If the bounded correction is infeasible or exceeds the bank's held-out `p99.99` value/slope/topology envelope, zero the affected connected soft region and report it; never fall back to a repeated local bubble. Remasking after projection is forbidden because it would break the mean constraint.

Run the projection on complete haloed 1 m blocks and crop only afterward. Gates before quantization, computed in float64:

- max absolute `A(H)-Y < 1e-6 m`;
- canonical float64 samples from independently scheduled overlapping execution windows agree within `1e-10 m`;
- no one-meter PSD/gradient quilting above the held-out envelope;
- every hard-mask residual is exactly zero before height addition;
- process-specific amplitude and shape diagnostics remain inside the calibrated held-out envelope after projection;
- connected crest/pit/mound geometry, curvature-sign topology, persistence/size, and joint height-slope-curvature statistics remain inside the calibrated held-out envelope;
- if projection removes more than 35% of a process module's band energy, that module/profile is invalid and must be redesigned rather than amplitude-normalized afterward.

### 9.7 Forbidden normalization

Do not normalize the final residual to a target global or class RMS. Amplitudes, widths, density, anisotropy, and decay come from fitted distributions with units and provenance. Final clipping is allowed only as an error guard at the empirical `p99.99` envelope and must increment a failure counter; clipping more than `0.01%` of valid samples fails the tile.

### 9.8 Structural terrain clusters

Structural terrain is a separate output of `asset-gen`, not a displacement layered onto the heightfield. A versioned outcrop-family manifest binds 3D source meshes/point clouds, coordinate/unit transforms, lithology and weathering labels, observed feature distributions, ground/collar masks, license, and source hashes. The generator receives factor fields and a base-height collar, produces final metric triangles in EPSG:3301-relative coordinates, and bakes normals/tangents/material coordinates, collision, and vegetation-exclusion footprints. A cook-side Node step runs the repository's existing `BuildDag.ts` boundary-locking QEM and `meshletizeDag`; runtime must never call DAG construction, clusterization, or a morphology generator.

The Taevaskoda structural seed is already present in ETAK `E_102_nolv_j`: `etak_id=1829131`, `Looduslik järsak`, `kaldaastang=Jah`, 3D crown-line length `191.26 m`, bounds `E679694.524..679824.294`, `N6444786.802..6444861.897`, crown elevations `50.163..68.305 m`. This line is evidence and deterministic feature ownership, not a complete wall. Fetch the intersecting official classified LAZ 1:2000 sheets for crown/foot and exposed-ground evidence. Top-down ALS may support a conservative wall shell but cannot establish deep recesses or overhangs; those require terrestrial/mobile TLS or registered multi-view oblique imagery.

Ownership rules:

- One globally aligned structural cell owns each connected outcrop; support may cross height chunks and structural cells.
- A conservative collar is single-valued in plan view and exactly samples the decoded packed height surface. Structural vertices on its outer boundary equal that surface within `1 mm`; normals transition over a recipe-pinned collar width.
- Inside the ownership footprint, the structural mesh owns visible terrain and collision. Height residuals are zero, terrain triangles are clipped or depth-hidden by the cooked footprint, and trees/understory/grass/debris are rejected or regrounded against the structural surface according to a cooked consumer mask.
- The mesh must be manifold over the visible/collision domain, have no inverted or degenerate triangles, close caves/recesses deliberately, and provide a watertight transition to the heightfield. It may not expose a hole when its clusters are not resident; demand must refine from a packed height-compatible proxy or keep the parent representation visible until structural clusters are ready.
- Structural faces use a new explicit-mesh `cliff` material class whose resolve reads triangle/packed normals. They may temporarily use the existing explicit `rock` branch for a plumbing fixture, but may never use terrain material class `terrain`, whose normal comes from `TerrainField` rather than the explicit mesh.
- Geometry, cluster hierarchy, material binding, collision proxy, footprint, collar, and source provenance share one recipe digest and transactional publication boundary.

The cheapest structural proof uses the full ETAK feature above so the wall crosses two 128 m structural cells and exercises a real page/chunk seam. Build one general `sandstone_wall_v1` family using licensed oblique SfM/TLS or a deliberately hand-authored non-production fixture. Rendering may prove the packed architecture before collision ships, but production requires a cooked collision proxy/BVH and capsule/walkable-surface queries because the current scalar `GroundProbe(x,z)` cannot collide with walls or select among overlapping surfaces. ## 11. Cook and canonical hierarchy

### 11.1 New cook boundary

Create `asset-gen/src/assetgen/cook/micro_height_cook.py`. Do not insert micro synthesis into `_cook_one` for every height LOD.

Pipeline:

1. Resolve and fingerprint inputs.
2. Create isolated staging root `data/work/builds/<recipe_digest>/`.
3. Enumerate published LOD `-2/-1` keys and transient LOD `-2` support keys required by every `-1` parent.
4. Solve the conservative base over the full LOD `-1` rectangle plus support and build covariate caches by aligned 256 m execution window plus 32 m operator halo.
5. Generate the canonical LOD `-2` master surface over that complete synthesis extent.
6. Split it into 128 m chunks, including one transient child-chunk column east, one row south, and the southeast corner outside the LOD `-1` rectangle for parent aprons.
7. Quantize and decode every published or transient LOD `-2` support chunk identically; pack only keys inside declared published LOD `-2` rectangles.
8. Assemble each complete 4x4 child group from decoded LOD `-2`, box-reduce by 4, and write LOD `-1`.
9. Verify LOD `-1` reduced by 4 against the existing decoded LOD0 authority.
10. Run all structural, statistical, and release gates.
11. Record a Merkle root of every transient quantized array/header in parent sidecars, discard transient payload files only after verification, and build a non-latest preview manifest.

LOD `-1` is never generated by the morphology code.

### 11.2 Coverage and parent closure

For each height chunk `(l,cx,cz)` where `l < 0`, its parent is:

```text
(l+1, floor_div(cx,4), floor_div(cz,4))
```

Use mathematical floor division for negative coordinates. Publication requires recursive parent existence through LOD0: every `-2` has its `-1` parent and every `-1` has its LOD0 parent.

Version 1 published coverage is a complete rectangle independently at each negative rung. Every published LOD `-2` rectangle is aligned to complete `512 m` LOD `-1` footprints, so a present `-1` parent has either all 16 published fine children inside a declared fine rectangle or none. A broader published LOD `-1` rectangle may therefore exist without published LOD `-2`; this is the Stage 4 serving model. Those parent-only areas are still synthesized transiently at canonical LOD `-2`, quantized, decoded, and reduced by the sole Section 11.4 path. They save serving/storage cost, not generator CPU cost. Arbitrary published holes inside either rectangle are forbidden. Sparse inherited layers such as water and records retain exactly the keys listed by the approved base manifest and are not subjected to a rectangular-coverage rule.

The existing LOD0-4 country floor remains present. Fine coverage augments it; it never replaces it.

### 11.3 Apron ownership

Core ownership is half-open in sample-cell space. The west/north chunk owns its 2048 core samples. East/south fine aprons come from the canonical neighboring master core, including transient outside-coverage support at the outer edge; they are not regenerated and never zeroed. A parent core uses 4x4 decoded child cores. Its east/south/corner aprons reduce the first four decoded core samples in the transient neighboring child column/row/corner, not the single fine apron sample. Parent sidecars store the ordered child and transient-support digests used for both core and apron.

Each chunk retains the existing integer-meter `qoffset = floor(min(height)-1)`. Allowed decimal qscales divide one meter mathematically, but their float32 header representation is not binary-exact. Adjacent chunks at the same LOD use the same declared qscale and share identical float64 canonical boundary samples before quantization. After decoding, each shared sample must satisfy:

```text
abs(a_dec - b_dec) <= 0.5*qscale_a + 0.5*qscale_b + 1e-6 m
```

The encoder must reject u16 range overflow rather than clamp it. LOD `-2` uses the selected fine qscale; LOD `-1` uses `parent_qscale_m` because its larger footprint may need more vertical range. Rendered seam normals and fixed grazing-light captures remain authoritative even when the numeric tolerance passes.

### 11.4 Quantized hierarchy order

The packed parent is derived from decoded children, whether those children are published or transient support:

```text
fine_q      = encode(H_minus2)
fine_dec    = decode(fine_q)
parent_real = box_mean_4(fine_dec)
parent_q    = encode(parent_real)
parent_dec  = decode(parent_q)
```

LOD0 remains the existing decoded authority. Verification compares decoded arrays using their declared qscales:

```text
max_abs(box4(parent_dec) - lod0_dec)
    <= 0.5*qscale_fine + 0.5*qscale_parent + 0.5*qscale_lod0 + 1e-6
```

For one `-1` core, reduce its `2048 x 2048` decoded core by 4 to the corresponding `512 x 512` subwindow of the decoded LOD0 core; aprons are verified separately. To verify a complete LOD0 core, assemble the `4 x 4` children from core arrays only into `8192 x 8192`, reduce by 4, and compare the resulting `2048 x 2048` array. A Stage 1 hero with one `-1` child compares only its exact LOD0 subwindow. The pre-quantized master must still satisfy the tighter Section 9 projection gate.

### 11.5 Waterbed order

The waterbed carve operates on the authoritative 1 m LOD0 before micro synthesis. Micro covariates receive the carved base and the water masks. Fine residual is exactly zero over open water, so fine children reduce to the carved LOD0 bed.

This does not make every raw DTM water sample trustworthy. The Suur Taevaskoda audit found a coherent Maa-amet ground-model/TIN bridge across the Ahja channel beside the steep cliff: a roughly `225 m2` false component reaches `52.22 m` and extends `13.45 m` into mapped water. The existing waterbed pass preserves that absolute ramp and subtracts only normal hydraulic depth. A corrected production base must therefore share one river-surface conditioning result between waterY and bed carving: derive longitudinal `S(s)` from robust low/central transverse samples tied to ETAK flow centerlines; reject coherent steep planar water-interior sections; interpolate only from trustworthy neighboring stations while retaining real longitudinal grade; set waterY to `S(s)`; and, inside a conservative fully wet core, use `min(rawDTM,S(s))-hydraulicDepth`. Taper correction to zero through a `2-3 m` shoreline uncertainty strip so legitimate cliff-bank terrain and every dry texel remain unchanged. Fail an untrustworthy reach rather than silently use raw DTM. This belongs in `process/water.py` and `layers_cook.py`, never the micro generator or runtime.

Pinned base `708478a57c2118ea` predates that correction. It remains valid for a clearly labeled local streaming/synthesis pilot, but its known Ahja artifact cannot pass final Taevaskoda visual acceptance or become the production authority. Correcting it requires a separately cooked, verified and user-approved base release; negative LODs may not silently disagree with inherited LOD0.

## 13. Runtime ingestion and terrain-DAG normalization

Runtime work is ingestion and sampling/drawing of packed terrain, not synthesis.

### 13.1 Height-level geometry

Refactor `PlaneFill.layerGeom` and the brain's `layerGeo` so a height level uses:

```text
t(l) = baseTexelMeters * lodStep^l
F(l) = grid.chunkMeters * lodStep^l
R    = grid.chunkRes
```

Each plane's integer index is in that level's own sample lattice. Its origin is:

```text
planeOriginX = grid.originX + (n0x + 0.5)*t(l)
planeOriginZ = grid.originZ + (n0z + 0.5)*t(l)
```

Chunk lookup for a level-index window is simply `floor_div(n,R)`. Do not express a negative physical stride as a fractional index in the 1 m lattice.

`chunksInWindow`, `copyChunkF32`, scroll placement, coverage bounds, clamp extension, and origin packets shall operate in the selected level's integer lattice. Unit tests compare every result to Section 5.2 world coordinates.

This exact per-level path is selected by manifest format 2. The format 1 planner remains unchanged for legacy releases and `GeneratedWorldSource`, which is required for parity. A format 2 release may reuse the compressed payload of an established positive LAC1 chunk, but its placement is governed by the explicit format 2 manifest geometry rather than the legacy format 1 offset convention.

Keep three distinct coverage concepts. `bootCenter` is the center of the finest published negative-LOD rectangle. `coverageByLod[l]` is the exact physical rectangle at one stored rung and governs eligibility/fallback. `renderDomain` is the inherited authority/coarse union clipped by view distance and expressed in the normalized finest lattice. Public terrain/material/vegetation coverage remains the inherited authority domain; adding a 512 m LOD `-2` hero must not shrink terrain, biome, soil, trees, water, canopy, shadows, or probes to that hero. Convert a world edge to a normalized fine edge only as `(worldEdge-grid.originAxis)/fineCell`, then round outward.

### 13.2 Normalized finest terrain lattice

Let:

```text
lf = min(height.lods) = -2
fineCell = baseTexelMeters * lodStep^lf = 0.0625 m
normalizedStride(l) = lodStep^(l-lf)
```

All normalized strides are integers:

| Height LOD | Normalized stride |
|---:|---:|
| `-2` | `1` |
| `-1` | `4` |
| `0` | `16` |
| `1` | `64` |

Integer stride does not imply coincident sample centers. For a finest-lattice index `nf`, source sampling is defined through world space:

```text
w                 = grid.origin + (nf + 0.5)*fineCell
g_l               = (w - grid.origin)/t(l) - 0.5
g_l from fine idx = (nf + 0.5)/normalizedStride(l) - 0.5
```

`g_l` is generally fractional: for stride 4 the phase is `1.5` fine samples. `PlaneFill`, CPU `heightAt`, tile baking, normals, skirts, and shader sampling must bilinearly sample the selected level at `g_l`; none may address a coarse plane with `nf/stride` integer truncation. The hierarchy guarantees area means, not point equality, so this phase rule is required even when reduction passes.

`StreamBrainClient` sends `tiles.cell = fineCell`, not 1 m. `StreamBrainCore` retains `baseStride = 1` in units of finest samples. The tile clipmap continues doubling its stride; source-level selection chooses the finest resident height plane whose sample spacing is no coarser than the tile stride.

This adds four binary clipmap levels between the old 1 m base and the new `0.0625 m` base. With `gridN=128` and `tilesPerSide=4`, the finest ring spans about 32 m, then expands geometrically. That is the intended near-only demand behavior and must be measured rather than assumed cheap.

Do not allocate two additional full `2048^2` r32f windows. Version 1 starts with `1536^2` for both LOD `-2` (`96 m`) and LOD `-1` (`384 m`), retaining the established size policy for LOD0 and above. These cover the 64 m and 256 m outer consumer rings that still require each source, plus frozen Stage-1 lead margins of 16 m and 64 m per side respectively for normals, skirts, and request latency. The two planes total about `18.9 MB` per retained copy: about `18.9 MB` GPU and `18.9 MB` worker, not `33.6 MB` each. The pure planner receives these versioned margins as configuration; it does not read live camera speed or latency telemetry. Captures measure whether the frozen margins cover the observed speed and p95 request latency. Starvation fails Stage 1 and requires an explicit recipe/config revision rather than silent dynamic resizing.

### 13.3 Source-level selection

For a tile stride `s` in finest samples, select:

```text
desired_m = s * fineCell
source_lod = max({ l present and resident | t(l) <= desired_m })
source_subsample = desired_m / t(source_lod)
```

The `max` is deliberate: select the coarsest resident source that is no coarser than the tile's desired spacing, not the first/finest containing plane. `source_subsample` must be an integer power of two for the current clipmap. If a fine level is outside its rectangular coverage or not yet resident, fall back atomically to the next coarser resident level for the whole tile. Never fill a missing fine window by clamping a fine chunk edge across the hole.

Define `minClipLevelForCoverage(tileBoundsExpandedByMargin)` before child nomination:

```text
0  if the expanded bounds are inside resident LOD -2 coverage
2  else if they are inside resident LOD -1 coverage
4  otherwise, using established LOD0+
```

The terrain partition tree may create or reserve a node only when its binary clip level is at least this minimum and the chosen source plane covers the entire expanded tile. Child nomination below the minimum is prohibited; the system must not repeatedly bake coarse samples onto fine-grid nodes. If residency changes, a teleport occurs, or the coverage predicate invalidates a pending bake, cancel it, retain the attached parent, and reevaluate. Use 15% refine/merge distance hysteresis and a 500 ms minimum residency dwell to prevent boundary oscillation.

Preserve the existing partition invariant that a region renders either its parent or its children, never both. A refine transition uses five prebuilt child DAG variants with `alpha=[0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1]`:

```text
edgeWeight = smoothstep(0, 1, minVertexRowsToChildBoundary / 4)
h(alpha)   = parentPackedSample + alpha*edgeWeight*(childPackedSample-parentPackedSample)
```

All samples come from resident packed planes through Section 13.2 world-coordinate sampling. `alpha=0` children reproduce the parent surface on the child lattice; `alpha=1` has full child relief in the interior and a four-row parent-matched edge. Build all four children for all five steps before transition. The existing mailbox atomically replaces parent with the complete `alpha=0` child set, then atomically replaces the child set at 62.5 ms intervals through `alpha=1`; at no frame are parent and children both live. Merge walks the variants in reverse and atomically replaces `alpha=0` children with the parent.

`PartitionTree` adds states `stable`, `refine-preparing`, `refine-morph`, and `merge-morph`, plus `transitionGeneration` and `activeMorphStep`. `TileBakeRequest` carries generation, parent source LOD, and morph step. Source planes and the parent handle remain pinned until the transition or reversal completes; mailbox acknowledgements advance a step, and stale generations are discarded. Teleport, eligibility loss, or cancellation before first attach leaves the parent unchanged. Cancellation after attach reverses from the acknowledged step to `alpha=0` before restoring the parent; it may not evict the packed inputs needed for reversal. The five-variant build/cache multiplier is reported in Stage 1 and is subject to the DAG build/cache budgets. This is runtime interpolation of packed heights, not synthesis. Fixed motion captures must show no crack, flash, or discernible relief pop; failure blocks publication.

### 13.4 DAG retention

`BuildHeightGrid` error metrics are in world meters and must be audited at the new cell size. Add an explicit `microMinRetainedBand` gate:

- a fixture with `0.02/0.05/0.10/0.20 m` features at `0.125-1 m` wavelengths is packed and built;
- emitted geometry is sampled or rasterized back at the fixed review camera;
- features at or above the selected visual threshold survive simplification;
- triangle/cluster counts and build time remain within Section 16 budgets.

Do not lower simplification globally without the fixture and performance evidence. A near-level-specific error policy is allowed because it changes retention, not height synthesis.

### 13.5 Fine coverage boundaries

Each LOD `-2` coverage component is a complete 512 m-aligned rectangle. LOD `-1` has its own complete rectangle and may be broader. Runtime exposes both exact world boxes. Fine rings refine only where the tile plus margin lies inside the applicable box; elsewhere `minClipLevelForCoverage` begins from the finest available coarser rung. At a boundary:

- child surface reduces to the parent;
- skirts remain a crack safety net, not the consistency mechanism;
- no zero/clamped fine samples are permitted;
- repeated crossings under camera motion must not oscillate or pop.

### 13.6 Grounding consumers

Do not use one fine height chunk to ground a 2048 m record chunk.

For static cooked tree records:

- recook tree records after micro height;
- add an absolute `y` f32 column whose value is the final instance-origin height `sampledGround - scale*0.12`, sampled from the finest packed surface covering the exact world `(x,z)`;
- record the height recipe digest in the record layer's build provenance;
- use the stored `y` verbatim at runtime and do not apply the tree sink a second time.

Until a tree chunk has the format-2 `y` column, its fallback derivation queries `TerrainField.heightAt` independently for each record point with the per-level coverage rule; it must not fetch or reuse the one finest chunk covering the center of the 2048 m record bin. Missing `y` is permitted for inherited Stage 1 records only and is forbidden in the Stage 3 production AOI.

ETAK boulder piles cannot use one record-level `y` because member positions and bed sinks are derived individually. Keep their existing deterministic layout, but sample the finest valid packed surface at every member `(x,z)` and then apply the established `EtakBoulders.ts` slope-scaled sink exactly once. Store the height recipe digest as boulder build provenance even though `y` remains derived.

For procedural scatter already performed by the runtime, such as grass and some understory:

- sampling the resident packed `TerrainField` at the exact root is allowed;
- the lookup must select the finest valid resident surface for that point;
- performance must be measured in the existing hot path before enabling it;
- guide-plane approximations must not visibly float or bury roots relative to the terrain mesh.

Fartile/far vegetation may use the coarser surface appropriate to its rendered grade. The same visible geometry and grounding grade must agree; do not fetch fine chunks for far impostors.

### 13.7 Generated source compatibility

`GeneratedWorldSource` keeps its established 2048 m grid and 1 m finest height. It reports `finestLod=0`, so normalized runtime math yields the exact previous `fineCell=1 m` behavior. Required parity test: generated-source manifest, chunk enumeration, record binning, terrain hashes, and fixed screenshots remain unchanged.

### 13.8 Structural terrain ingestion

Structural chunks enter the existing world demand/explicit-cluster-culling path as immutable prebuilt geometry. Runtime may decode, upload, cull, render, query the cooked collision proxy, and apply the cooked ownership/vegetation masks; it may not displace, erode, retopologize, scatter, call `RockGen`, or otherwise synthesize the cliff. Reuse explicit vertex fetch, DAG culling, HW/SW raster, shadows, HZB, StreamOrigin rebasing, content-addressed indexes, and the upload token bucket. Do not reuse the height tile pool (one-word grid vertices), boulder records (runtime `RockGen` parameters), or far-foliage voxel machinery.

Add an explicit mesh-page pool in `GeometryRegistry` (`reserveMeshPagePool`, attach/park/evict/rebase), a sparse 128 m `StructuralBand` with hysteresis and page budgets, and `enc=4` decoding/origin validation in the world source. Parent height-compatible proxy remains visible until every required child cluster and material is resident, then transitions through the same acknowledged-generation/XOR ownership discipline used for terrain refinement. Leaving coverage reverses the transition before eviction.

The heightfield remains authoritative outside the cooked ownership footprint and along its collar. Inside the footprint, depth ownership must prevent z-fighting and double surfaces. Shadows, normals/material coordinates, collision, grass, trees, understory, debris, water contact, and navigation must agree on the same representation owner. Failure of any consumer is a publication blocker, not a reason to hide the structural mesh in the material pass.

For every format-2 packed-terrain release, `NaniteFrame` must compile `TerrainDisp` out of terrain fetch, shadows, and `NaniteGrass` root evaluation. The established displacement may remain for generated worlds and legacy format-1 releases. Fixed captures and shader/pipeline diagnostics must prove that no camera-distance fade changes packed terrain geometry between `45-85 m`.

## 14. Transactional build and publication

### 14.1 Isolated directories

```text
data/work/builds/<recipe_digest>/
  plan.json
  inputs/
    base-manifest.json
    base-index/
  covariates/
  chunks/<layer>/<lod>/

  m/<manifest_hash>/...

data/out/c/...                       # canonical immutable content store
data/out/m/<manifest_hash>/...       # published immutable manifests/indexes
```

The global legacy `data/work/chunks` is never an input to a format 2 release.

### 14.2 Plan manifest

`plan.json` freezes:

- exact LOD `-2` and LOD `-1` rectangle bounds after their declared alignment;
- every expected published key per layer and LOD plus every transient LOD `-2` support key;
- every required parent relationship;
- approved base-manifest path, SHA-256, every inherited index SHA-256, and every inherited key/content hash;
- source and recipe hashes;
- qscales and codecs;
- expected country-floor keys;
- allowed optional/absent chunks;
- estimated resource range.

Chunk sidecars store the recipe digest, input-window digest, chunk key, qoffset, qscale, generator version, payload SHA-256, and for parents the ordered child/support Merkle root. Resume validates all fields. Transient support keys are not emitted into a release index and are removed only after their dependent parents and verification report are complete.

### 14.3 Completeness gate

Before writing `COMPLETE`:

- dense-height actual key sets equal their planned rectangles exactly;
- sparse/non-height actual key sets equal the inherited base indexes exactly unless a separately planned recook explicitly replaces that layer;
- no unexpected/stale key exists;
- all LAC2 headers match path key, res, origin, layer, and encoding; sidecars, `plan.json`, and the packaged manifest carry and agree on recipe identity because the 56-byte header has no recipe field;
- every negative-height child has complete recursive parents; a `-1` parent is not required to have `-2` children outside declared `-2` rectangles;
- LOD0-4 country floor required by the release is present;
- cross-layer physical extents match their declared grid geometry;
- every hard verification gate passes.

### 14.4 Atomic publish

Chunk URLs remain rooted at `/c/<layer>/<lod>/...`, exactly as the current client expects; format 2 adds no implicit build-relative content path. `build --no-latest` first imports the complete approved base index, overlays planned replacement keys, writes each new immutable content object to canonical `data/out/c` using create-or-verify semantics, reopens it by its production root URL, and only then writes the full preview manifest/index tree under `data/out/builds/<digest>/m`. Unreferenced content objects are safe to create before publication. The preview loader receives that manifest path explicitly, resolves indexes relative to its manifest directory, and resolves chunks from root `/c`. Stage 1 therefore produces a loadable full base release plus 16 LOD `-2` chunks and one LOD `-1` chunk, not a partial 17-chunk world. `publish`:

1. verifies `COMPLETE` and `verify.json` hashes;
2. verifies every referenced root `/c` object exists and matches index size/hash;
3. copies or promotes the immutable manifest/index tree to `data/out/m/<manifest_hash>`;
4. re-opens the release through the production parser and rechecks counts;
5. atomically replaces `latest.json` only as the final operation.

No command that cooks chunks may update `latest.json`.

## 15. Verification and acceptance

### 15.1 Hard cook gates

All are mandatory:

- same recipe cooked with worker counts 1 and 4 produces identical uncompressed quantized u16 arrays and identical final files;
- cook order and resume produce identical files;
- transient child/support quantized arrays and parent Merkle roots are identical across worker counts and resume;
- pre-quantized LOD `-2` reduces to source LOD0 within `1e-6 m`;
- decoded hierarchy meets Section 11.4 tolerance;

- no NaN/Inf occurs in valid output;
- one-way parent closure and exact per-rung rectangular coverage pass;
- all headers, origins, paths, and manifest formulas pass sample-center oracle tests;
- a fine-coverage hole cannot be represented in a verified rectangular release.

### 15.2 Morphology diagnostic gates

For each morphology family and wavelength band, compare generated review sites to held-out exemplars:

- amplitude quantiles `p05/p25/p50/p75/p95`;
- 2D PSD and radial PSD;
- semivariogram range and sill;
- anisotropy direction and ratio;
- slope and curvature distributions;
- joint height-slope-curvature distributions, curvature-sign topology, connected crest/pit/mound geometry, and feature persistence/size;
- event density, width, height/depth, aspect ratio, and orientation;
- spatial correlation with the conditioning factor that is supposed to cause it;
- repetition diagnostics from Section 9.3.

When a family has at least three independent held-out sites, generated median and interquartile statistics must lie inside the held-out 95% site-bootstrap interval and must preserve the observed within-site versus between-site variance. The one-site Lapinjarvi pilot cannot use a site bootstrap. It instead reports a spatial block bootstrap over non-overlapping `2 m x 2 m` holdout blocks, with blocks resampled rather than individual correlated texels, and labels the result `single-site foreign-analogue infrastructure diagnostic`. ### 16.1 Measured current experimental sizes

The noisy failed experiment averaged about `2.945 MB` compressed per fine chunk and `2.98 MB` per 0.25 m chunk. These are measurements of the failed generator, not forecasts. Correlated measured residuals may compress better; richer entropy or a finer qscale may compress worse.

For a version-1 parent-aligned `16,384 m x 16,384 m` Taevaskoja pilot rectangle, the planned counts are:

| Rung | Chunks | Experimental average | Estimated compressed total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `-2`, 0.0625 m | `16,384` | `2.945 MB` | `~48.25 GB` |
| `-1`, 0.25 m | `1,024` | `2.98 MB` | `~3.05 GB` |
| `0`, 1 m | `64` | existing | `~0.19 GB` measured |

The rejected dirty-grid forensic count remains `15,876` in Section 4 because that interrupted coverage was snapped differently; it is not the planned count. Country-wide extrapolation is roughly `8.3 TB` for LOD `-2` and `0.5 TB` for LOD `-1` at those averages. Version 1 must not auto-cook either nationally.

### 16.2 Required measurements

Report p50, p95, and worst case for:

- compressed bytes per chunk at every qscale candidate;
- synthesis CPU seconds per 256 m supertile;
- transient fine-support chunks generated/decoded per published `-1`, scratch peak bytes, and discarded bytes;
- peak worker RSS;
- decode time, decoder LRU bytes, transfer copies, and temporary decoded bytes; one `2049^2` f32 chunk is `16.79 MB` and four overlapping decoded chunks are about `67.2 MB` before copies;
- request latency and burst bytes during walking/teleport;
- TerrainField GPU and worker RAM;
- tile/DAG build time, cluster count, triangle count, and five-step morph-variant cache size;
- steady-state and peak frame time in the fine ring.

### 16.3 Provisional budgets

Stage 1 fails pending explicit user approval if:

- a synthesis worker exceeds `4 GB` RSS;
- aggregate synthesis RSS exceeds `12 GB`; worker count must be reduced from the measured per-worker peak rather than overcommitting the machine;
- a 256 m supertile exceeds `10 minutes` on the agreed reference CPU;
- p95 compressed fine chunk exceeds `6 MB`;
- fine-rung streaming adds more than `50 MB` transient network burst for ordinary walking;
- added retained TerrainField GPU memory exceeds `24 MB` for the two fine levels;
- added worker retained height windows exceed `24 MB`;
- decoder LRU plus in-flight decode/transfer copies exceeds `256 MB` or fails to return to budget after leaving coverage;
- ground-level p95 frame time regresses by more than `10%`;
- micro tile/DAG build causes a visible streaming stall above `50 ms` on the main thread.

The user may choose quality over a provisional budget, but the measurement cannot be omitted.

A haloed `5120^2` float64 height component is about `210 MB`; the implementation may hold at most one such transient component plus the two float64 accumulation planes unless a measured memory plan proves otherwise. Covariates may remain compact dtypes. Memmaps and sequential module accumulation are mandatory when the predicted peak exceeds the worker limit.

## 20. Default decisions and user checkpoints

The specification makes these defaults so implementation is not blocked by a decision menu:

1. Use signed physical height LODs `-2/-1` in format 2; keep the shared 2048 m grid.
2. Use deterministic measured exemplars plus causal event modules for single-valued ground and cook-packed structural clusters for non-heightfield outcrops; no diffusion in version 1.
3. Fetch geology before generator claims; fetch orthophoto only for enabled families where held-out ablation proves it useful.
4. Use one 512 m parent-closed hero area for Stage 1; do not bake the 16 km pilot immediately.
5. Start qscale at `2 mm`, subject to the required `1/2/5/10 mm` render/size ablation.
6. Preserve exact 1 m DTM cell means on the height path; do not force non-single-valued outcrops into that representation.

The user must approve only these material checkpoints:

- the Stage 1 ground-level visible result and selected qscale;
- the acquisition plan for local Estonian TLS/SfM exemplars;
- the final hero/pilot coverage and resulting storage/egress cost before Stage 4.

## 21. Definition of done

The task is done only when:

- the canonical global grid and every existing non-height layer retain their established meaning;
- an isolated verified format 2 release contains complete independent LOD `-2/-1` rectangles for the approved AOIs, one-way child-to-parent closure, and the complete inherited LOD0-4 floor and sparse layers;
- all height microtopography and structural terrain are generated cook-side and packed as final geometry;
- the generator uses factorized geology, soil, hydrology, land use, and measured exemplars rather than chunk classes or noise;
- LOD `-1` core and aprons are derived only from decoded published/transient LOD `-2` support, aprons are canonical, and LOD0 reconstruction passes;
- the runtime uses `0.0625 m` as the finest terrain cell only where valid data is resident and falls back without holes;
- the terrain DAG demonstrably retains the accepted micro feature bands;
- ground contacts agree with the visible surface;
- vertical, undercut, cave, or overhanging acceptance targets use verified structural clusters rather than a finer height ramp, with watertight collars, collision, materials, masks, demand, and proxy transitions;
- resource and serving costs are measured and approved;
- fixed ground-level A/B and motion reviews show an obvious preferred improvement with no seams, repetition, quilting, popping, or shimmer;
- only a complete verified build can be promoted to `latest`.

Anything less is an experiment, not a shipped microtopography system.
