## Preregistration And Leakage Record

The current spec's owner-selection preregistration did not exist before the historical LUKE quilt was designed, tuned, cooked, and visually inspected. That work is therefore permanently historical and rejected. It cannot be grandfathered into the future bakeoff, treated as a previous accepted owner, or supply frozen thresholds.

The Hovi Q1 screens and Evo floor-sheet estimator were also inspected before a target-specific specialist preregistration. They remain valid acquisition/conversion engineering evidence because they make no owner or synthesis claim. They are not blind evidence. Any site, plot, threshold, filter, or failure mode inspected during these passes belongs on the development side of a future split, and a later preregistration may not retroactively label it untouched.

The sealed `JS_SPRUCE1` Järvselja plot remains the only recorded untouched Estonia candidate. Preserve it byte-uninspected until preregistration is approved. If it is opened for conversion or semantic diagnosis before that freeze, it becomes development evidence and a new untouched blind site is required.

No current candidate artifact has entered a production release, so the preregistration deviation has not contaminated an accepted morphology owner. The remedy is prospective: freeze the next experiment before training or candidate comparison, not rewrite history or weaken the gate.

## Remaining Gate Order

1. Close the Hovi engineering transaction honestly: either perform and verify the promised atomic publication, or retain the current manifest explicitly as staging-only. This does not advance its scientific status.
2. Materialize scan-balanced, view-aware Hovi raw hypotheses and comparable Evo diagnostics with support, visibility, full-versus-thinned, inter-scan disagreement, and semantic-unknown masks. Do not fill unsupported cells or call them target surfaces.
3. Perform human semantic QA and independent error/support qualification. Freeze include/exclude policy for mineral soil, peat/moss, roots, litter, clasts, deadwood, living vegetation, water, and objects. If B1/B2 signal cannot be separated from measurement and interpolation error, record `target_evidence_insufficient` and stop that regime.
4. Define one exact first-regime claim and assemble at least three qualified independent sites across at least two campaigns. Hyytiälä `HY_SPRUCE4` and `HY_PINE2` do not count as independent sites. Evo plots do not count until their semantics/error contract passes. FORWARD and Biala Gora do not fill a forest-floor split merely because they contain terrain.
5. Complete the checked physical-analogue dossier, Estonia condition envelope, OOD abstention rule, and fixed Estonia sentinels. Foreign-only evidence may authorize at most a user-accepted `neighbor_analogue_only` pilot, never national coverage.
6. Freeze the target registry, geographic/campaign split, leakage audit, condition strata, exact tensors/operators/windows, training configuration, seeds, metrics, real-vs-real margins, render protocol, cost ceiling, and valid no-owner outcomes in a user-approved target-specific preregistration. Keep Järvselja sealed until this point.
7. Run the required corrected-only, exemplar, conditional-simulation, process where applicable, deterministic-regressor, GAN, diffusion, and justified-hybrid challengers. Omit a family only through a preregistered evidence-based justification. Quality determines survival; cost only breaks a quality-equivalent tie.
8. Select an owner only after hard failures, real-vs-real morphology noninferiority, site/campaign-independent statistics, memorization/repetition checks, and frozen blind rendered preference all pass. Otherwise publish no morphology for that regime.
9. Only for an accepted owner, compose one absolute 0.0625 m master, derive LOD -1/0 parents from decoded children, preserve masks and inherited layers, measure packed/cook/serving cost, publish immutably, pass independent hierarchy verification and exact real-WebGPU boot, then request user visual judgment.
10. Repeat qualification and owner selection for every additional common Estonia regime before widening the claim. National publication remains a separate explicit user decision after measured multi-parent cost and serving evidence.

## No Silent Credit

- Dense raw occupancy is not target truth.
- A complete byte/scan-preserving materialization is not a terrain surface.
- A sparse floor estimate is not support-complete because its cell pitch is 6.25 cm.
- Multiple plots from one campaign do not satisfy the two-campaign gate.
- FORWARD and Biala Gora do not become forest-floor validation by adjacency of topic.
- The accepted structural WebGPU boot does not accept future morphology.
- Järvselja is not available for exploratory tuning merely because it is public.
- No exact up/downsample, seam, quantization, or packing result can waive visible noise, wrong process character, weak detail, or unsupported filler.

This audit does not change the normative spec. It records the present conformance boundary and the only admissible route from retained observations to a morphology-bearing Estonia preview.
