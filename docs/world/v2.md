# Estonian Geospatial & Environmental Data Catalogue for a 1:1 Physical Reconstruction of Estonia

## TL;DR
- **Estonia is one of the best open-geodata countries on earth: essentially every layer you need for a ground-truth reconstruction exists free under the Maa-amet open-data licence (CC BY 4.0 equivalent) via WFS/WMS/WCS and bulk download**, complemented by EELIS/Keskkonnaportaal (nature), Eesti Geoloogiateenistus (geology), and Copernicus/ESA (pan-European fill-ins).
- The best "what is physically at this location" backbone is: **Maa-amet 1 m DTM + 1 m nDSM + 4 m CHM** (terrain & canopy), **ETAK** (topographic land cover + hydrography), **Mullastikukaart 1:10 000** (soils — a vector map, **not** a 1 m raster), **Metsaregister + metsakasvukohatüüp** (forest stands + ground-flora proxy), and **EELIS** (habitats, protected areas, semi-natural communities).
- Plan around four real gaps: the soil map excludes cities/water/islets; standwise forest inventory is out of date on much private land (SMI is a statistical sample, not wall-to-wall); the 1:50 000 geological base map covers only ~55–60 of ~100 sheets; and lake **bathymetry** exists for only a few hundred lakes (mostly digitised historical charts), so most water depths must be modelled.

## Key Findings

**Coordinate systems.** All Maa-amet data is in **L-EST97 / EPSG:3301** (planar) with heights in **EH2000** (Baltic/European vertical reference) for everything issued since 1 January 2018 (earlier BK77). EELIS GeoServer nature data is also L-EST97. Copernicus/ESA global products are EPSG:4326 (WGS84).

**Licensing.** The dominant licence is the Maa- ja Ruumiamet open-data licence, explicitly a "Creative Commons vaste CC BY 4.0" (CC BY 4.0 equivalent), requiring attribution such as "Eesti topograafia andmekogu, Maa- ja Ruumiamet 2025". EELIS data: "EELIS (Estonian Nature Information System), Estonian Environment Agency". EGT geology: "Eesti geoloogiline baaskaart, Eesti Geoloogiateenistus [year]". Copernicus/ESA: free/open with attribution. **E-OBS is restricted to non-commercial research/education** — a licensing hazard for a commercial game world.

**WFS query cap.** All Maa-amet and EELIS GeoServer WFS services return **max 5000 objects per query** — download whole layers with feature paging (QGIS "enable feature paging", page size 5000).

## Details by Thematic Domain

### 1. SOILS
**Mullastikukaart / Digital Soil Map of Estonia**
- **Producer:** Maa-amet (historically the land-valuation "maa kvaliteedi ja hindamise kaart"). Distributor contact: avaandmed@maaamet.ee.
- **Scale/resolution:** **1:10 000 vector database** — NOT a 1 m raster (metadata denominator = 10000; maps originally at 1:5000 were generalised to 1:10 000). The "1 m" the user anchored on is a misconception: the soil map is a 1:10 000 polygon layer; the 1 m data at a location is the separate LiDAR DTM.
- **Attributes:** soil contours with type codes (šifrid), texture/mechanical composition (lõimis), humus- and peat-horizon thickness, stoniness (kivisus) classes, boniteet (quality score); soil-type semantics in the explanatory note (geoportaal.maaamet.ee/docs/muld/mullakaardi_seletuskiri.pdf).
- **Coverage:** covers almost all Estonia **except built-up urban areas, water bodies, and barren islets** ("andmed puuduvad linnade, veealade ja mullastikuta laidude kohal").
- **Access:** WMS + WFS (GML) via Maa-amet; INSPIRE Soil theme; archived at datadoi.ee (handle 33/103).
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0 equivalent.
- **Ranked #1 (soils):** Mullastikukaart 1:10 000 is the definitive per-location soil type/texture/drainage source; fill urban/water gaps with the geology Quaternary (pinnakate) map.

### 2. ELEVATION & TERRAIN
**Kõrgusmudelid / Elevation models (Maa-amet)** — geoportaal.maaamet.ee/est/ruumiandmed/korgusandmed/korgusmudelid-p508.html
- **DTM (maapinna kõrgusmudel / DEM):** 1 m, 5 m, 10 m, 25 m grid; GeoTIFF (32-bit) + XYZ ASCII; heights in EH2000. Whole-Estonia bulk files (10 m ≈ 2 GB, 5 m ≈ 7.7 GB, 25 m ≈ 343 MB).
- **DSM (maakatte kõrgusmudel):** 1 m + 5 m, whole country; heights above sea level.
- **nDSM (normalised DSM):** 1 m + 5 m — heights above ground (DSM − DTM); ideal for vegetation/building heights.
- **CHM (taimkatte kõrgusmudel / Canopy Height Model):** 4 m (from 2017 new-LiDAR areas) and 10 m (older, to 2016); class breaks 1–4/4–10/10–20/20–30/>30 m at 0.5 m steps; assumes no Estonian tree >50 m; buildings removed but may persist in dense urban areas.
- **Derivatives:** hillshade (reljeefivarjutus), colour relief, slope (nõlvakalded), contours (samakõrgusjooned), height points, water-level-rise model.
- **Vertical accuracy:** Maa-amet publishes the QA method, not a single RMSE — vertical control points measured by RTK-GNSS (ESTPOS) with quality std. dev. ≤3 cm used in adjustment; planimetric accuracy is explicitly **not** measured ("Plaanilist täpsust ... mõõdetud ei ole"). No discrete "RMSE = X m" is officially published; treat any such figure as unconfirmed.
- **Access:** WMS/WCS/WFS + bulk (korgusandmed-alla-p614.html) by map sheet.
- **Ranked #1 (terrain):** 1 m DTM for bare earth; add 1 m nDSM for object heights and 4 m CHM for canopy. New national LiDAR is released each December–January.

### 3. LIDAR / POINT CLOUD
**Aerolaserskaneerimise kõrguspunktid (Maa-amet)** — p499.html
- **Sensors:** Leica ALS50-II (2008–2016); Riegl VQ-1560i (from 2016/2017).
- **Point density (official):** older nationwide (Leica, 2400 m, 2008–2016) avg **0.45 pt/m²**, max spacing between adjacent points **≈ 2.6 m**; older dense-settlement (1500 m) **1.56 pt/m²**; 2016 transition (flight height 2400→3000 m for 25 cm orthophoto) dropped nationwide density **0.45 → 0.15 pt/m²**; post-2017 Riegl VQ-1560i ≈ **2.1 pt/m² spring**, **≈ 0.8 pt/m² summer/forestry** (UT thesis citing Maa-amet 2023); **low-altitude city flights (madallend, 1200 m): 18 pt/m²**.
- **Classification:** automatic (ground, vegetation, buildings).
- **Format & tiling:** **LAZ 1.4**; downloadable by the **1:2000 map-sheet grid** (official term — not literally "1 km² tiles").
- **Update:** ~one quarter of Estonia re-flown per year; larger settlements yearly.
- **Ranked #1 (LiDAR):** use raw point cloud only for custom derivatives (e.g., understory structure in madallend cities); otherwise use pre-made DTM/DSM/nDSM/CHM to save compute.

### 4. HYDROGRAPHY
- **ETAK hydrography (Maa-amet):** topographic backbone — vooluveekogud (rivers/streams line + polygon), seisuveekogud (lake/standing-water polygons), meri (sea), kaldajooned (shorelines), ditches, culverts, hydrographic structures; part of ETAK's 39-layer WFS, updated daily; bulk at laadi-etak-andmed-alla-p609.html. Caveat: ETAK does not distinguish public vs internal waters, and along the Russian border the control line runs through many water bodies.
- **EELIS / Keskkonnaregister water layers (Keskkonnaagentuur):** vesikonnad (river-basin districts), veekogumid (WFD water bodies), lake/river registry, abstractions, dams. GeoServer WFS/WMS at **https://gsavalik.envir.ee/geoserver/eelis/ows**; single-layer example `?service=WFS&version=1.0.0&request=GetFeature&outputFormat=application/json&typeName=eelis:kr_allikas`. Category I/II species locations withheld.
- **Watersheds:** via geology (põhjavee veelahkmed 1:50 000) and EELIS vesikonnad.
- **Bathymetry (batümeetria) — MAJOR GAP.** Digital depth maps exist only for the largest water bodies (Peipsi, Lämmijärv, Ülemiste, Emajõgi). TLÜ (Tallinn University Institute of Ecology) digitised ~200 historical charts (1906–2019, KIK-funded) and, since 2022, TLÜ + Keskkonnaagentuur hydrology dept run multibeam-sonar monitoring (WFD requirement: each water body every 30–50 yr); 2019–2024 projects mapped batches of ~13–15 small lakes each (Aheru, Saadjärv, Kuremaa, Vagula, etc.), with roughly 530 depth charts compiled overall. Marine/coastal: **EMODnet Bathymetry** harmonised DTM for Baltic regions; navigational charts (ENCs) from **Transpordiamet** for navigable waters (Narva, Peipsi, Emajõgi, Võrtsjärv, Pärnu, etc.).
- **Ranked #1 (hydrography):** ETAK for surface geometry of every lake/river/shoreline; overlay EELIS veekogumid for registry/basin attributes; TLÜ bathymetry where available, EMODnet for the sea, and expect to model depths elsewhere.

### 5. LAND COVER / LAND USE
- **ETAK kõlvikud (land cover):** national wall-to-wall polygons — puistud (forest), rohumaad/lagealad (grassland/open), haritav maa (arable), turbaväljad (peat fields), põõsastikud (shrub), inimtekkelised alad (artificial), vooluveed, seisuveed, meri, õuealad. Highest-resolution national land-cover layer; updated daily.
- **CORINE Land Cover (Estonia):** produced for Estonia by **KAUR/Keskkonnaagentuur**; 44-class 3-level nomenclature; MMU 25 ha (5 ha for CLC Change); reference years 1990/2000/2006/2012/2018; KAUR CLC WMS; pan-European raster 100 m + vector via Copernicus/EEA.
- **ESA WorldCover 10 m:** 2020 v100 (overall accuracy **74.4%**) and 2021 v200 (overall accuracy **76.7%**, validated by Wageningen University using 1.93 million sampling units at 21,624 locations); 11 classes from Sentinel-1/2; COG tiles EPSG:4326; free with attribution.
- **Copernicus HRL:** Tree Cover Density (10 m, 0–100%; 2018 ~96% OA at 30% threshold; 2021 update), Dominant Leaf Type (DLT), Forest Type (FTY), imperviousness, grassland, water & wetness — pan-European, cover Estonia, free/open.
- **Ranked #1 (land cover):** ETAK kõlvikud for national ground truth; use ESA WorldCover / Copernicus HRL for consistent raster classes or continuous canopy-density fields.

### 6. FOREST & VEGETATION
- **Metsaregister (KAUR forest register)** [user-anchored]: eraldis polygons + eraldis_element attributes; WFS at **https://gsavalik.envir.ee/geoserver/metsaregister/ows**. Standwise inventory. Caveat: since 1992 only state forests are inventoried on the classic 10-yr cycle; standwise data for much of the forest is outdated — "paiguti on andmed nüüdseks enam kui 20 aastat vanad" (in places now >20 years old), and private-forest coverage is incomplete.
- **metsakasvukohatüüp (forest site type)** [user-anchored]: typology usable as a proxy for understory/ground flora via indicator species.
- **Statistiline metsainventeerimine / National Forest Inventory (SMI)** — Keskkonnaagentuur, since 1999. Systematic sample on an 800×800 m tract ("trakt") grid, **16 plots per tract with measurement points every 200 m**; ~**3,000 forest-land sample plots and 4,000 model trees measured per year** (KAUR/Enn Pärt via ERR); permanent plots re-measured every 5 years; annual results are 5-year rolling. Statistically robust for national/regional totals but **not a per-location wall-to-wall map** (unsuitable for rare/small features). Annual tables (xlsx/pdf) on Keskkonnaportaal.
- **ALS-based forest height & growing-stock maps:** Arumäe & Lang (Estonian University of Life Sciences / Tartu Observatory) from Maa-amet 2016–2019 flights.
- **National single-tree vector model** (ee.maaamet.als-lod0-yksikpuud) [user-anchored]: ALS crown shapes + trunk position; tree type from Sentinel-2 NDVI; cities only.
- **European 10 m tree-species maps:** Blickensdörfer et al. 2024 (Remote Sensing of Environment 304:114069, DOI 10.1016/j.rse.2024.114069) — Sentinel-1/2 + German NFI, 11 species classes; German-only but the methodological reference. **ForestPaths European Tree Genus Map 2020** (De Keersmaecker et al. 2025, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.13341104) covers Estonia at genus level. Copernicus HRL Dominant Leaf Type gives conifer/broadleaf split for Estonia.
- **Ranked #1 (forest):** Metsaregister eraldis where current; fill stale/missing private stands with ALS CHM + Sentinel-derived products; use SMI for calibration/aggregate truthing; metsakasvukohatüüp for ground-flora inference.

### 7. HABITATS & BIOTOPES
- **EELIS inventeeritud elupaigad / habitat types (Keskkonnaagentuur):** Natura 2000 habitat-type polygons with EU Habitats Directive codes (e.g., 7110* raised bogs, 6450 alluvial meadows, 9080* fen woods, 3160 humic lakes). Via EELIS GeoServer WFS/WMS. Also vääriselupaigad (woodland key habitats, Forest Act §23).
- **Semi-natural communities / pärandkooslused (PLK):** Keskkonnaregister PLK layer (KKR_PLK) + Pärandkoosluste Kaitse Ühing (PKÜ) inventory; classes loopealsed/alvars, puisniidud/wooded meadows, rannaniidud/coastal meadows, lamminiidud/alluvial meadows, aruniidud, nõmmeniidud, soostunud niidud, puiskarjamaad. Public in Maa-amet X-GIS 2; combined layer rasterised to 20×20 m in the 2020 Tartu Ülikool ecological-functioning assessment (Helm & Toussaint 2020). ~700 plant species depend on them; best-preserved concentration in western Estonia.
- **Mires/peatlands (Estonian Mire Inventory):** Paal & Leibak 2011/2013 ("Estonian Mires Inventory of Habitats") — digital mire conservation-value database feeding EELIS mire records. Roughly one-quarter of Estonian peatland is still active mire; ~22% of Estonia is peat-covered but about three-quarters is artificially drained (Paal & Leibak 2011). The frequently cited 642,200 → 232,900 ha decline and ~240,000 ha remaining (5.3%) are widely quoted but not independently confirmed here — treat those exact figures as provisional. ELF ran an interim re-inventory (~460 sites; ~1400 with outdated data). Peat/turbaala definitions tie to the soil map (turvasmuld = peat >30 cm).
- **EUNIS:** Estonian habitats are cross-walked to EU codes; EUNIS-level classification derives from the Paal (1997) Estonian habitat classification and Natura mapping.
- **Ranked #1 (habitats):** EELIS inventeeritud elupaigad + KKR_PLK for mapped habitat/community polygons; Mire Inventory for peatland type/condition. These are inventory-based (not exhaustive), and category I/II sensitive layers are access-restricted.

### 8. SPECIES OCCURRENCE
- **eElurikkus / eBiodiversity (elurikkus.ee), on PlutoF (University of Tartu Natural History Museum):** aggregates specimens, observations, sounds, images, DNA-based records. Live counts (July 2026): **>7,000,000 occurrences and 45,353 species**, 6 data resources, 6 data partners. Feeds and is fed by GBIF; public taxon pages give per-species occurrence maps and Red List / protection categories.
- **GBIF:** Estonian datasets (incl. PlutoF reference-based occurrences) downloadable with DOIs in Darwin Core.
- **Atlases & specialist data:** Estonian plant distribution atlas (Kukk & Kull), breeding-bird atlas (Estonian Ornithological Society), invasive/võõrliigi leiukohad (Heracleum, Impatiens glandulifera) via EELIS info page.
- **Caveat:** category I & II protected species locations withheld from public EELIS services; occurrence data are presence-biased by sampling effort.
- **Ranked #1 (species):** GBIF/eElurikkus bulk download to seed species per biotope; treat as presence records, not wall-to-wall truth.

### 9. GEOLOGY
**Eesti Geoloogiateenistus (EGT) / Maa-amet — Geoloogiline baaskaart 1:50 000**
- **Content:** aluspõhi (bedrock), pinnakate (Quaternary/superficial), hüdrogeoloogia (groundwater), põhjavee kaitstus (groundwater vulnerability), plus geomorphology, bedrock relief, Quaternary thickness, mineral resources, geophysics (aeromagnetic + gravity anomalies), factual points (boreholes, wells, outcrops).
- **Coverage:** 1:50 000 is the optimal national scale; mapping began 1980s. **As of 1 Dec 2023, 55 base-map sheets are partially/fully covered by the digital base map**; more broadly, of ~100 mainland sheets geological data exists for ~60 but much is not yet digitised. Some objects (aeromagnetic anomalies, erratic boulders, springs/karst, stratotypes, boreholes) are viewable Estonia-wide. Producer: OÜ Eesti Geoloogiakeskus until end-2017, then EGT from 2018.
- **Also:** Eesti geoloogilised kaardid 1:400 000 (bedrock, crystalline basement, hydrogeology) cover the whole country — coarser, Eesti Geoloogiakeskus 1997–2006.
- **Access endpoints (confirmed):**
  - **WCS 50×50 m grid models:** `https://teenus.maaamet.ee/ows/wcs-geoloogia` — three interpolated grids: pinnakatte paksus (Quaternary thickness), settelise aluspõhja reljeef (sedimentary bedrock relief = LiDAR DTM minus Quaternary-thickness model, in EH2000), kristalse aluskorra reljeef (crystalline basement relief). NB: interpolated — actual subsurface may differ significantly.
  - **WMS:** `https://teenus.maaamet.ee/ows/wms-geoloogia`
  - **WFS:** 1:50 000 base-map layers via WFS; 1:400 000 at `https://teenus.maaamet.ee/ows/geoloogia400k`; mineral deposits `https://teenus.maaamet.ee/ows/maardlad`.
  - **INSPIRE GE:** inspire.maaamet.ee ArcGIS + inspire.geoportaal.ee/geoserver/GE_puurauk.
- **Downloads/formats:** vector (ESRI gdb, shp, MapInfo tab) + PDF open data via geoportaal.maaamet.ee and egt.ee (gis.egt.ee/portal); drill-core DB extract as CSV (monthly); mineral registry updated each working day.
- **CRS:** EPSG:3301 (L-EST97), EPSG:4258 (ETRS89). **Licence:** cite EGT.
- **Ranked #1 (geology):** the 1:50 000 pinnakate (Quaternary) map for surficial materials where it exists, plus the WCS Quaternary-thickness and bedrock-relief grids for 3D subsurface; fall back to 1:400 000 outside the 55-sheet footprint.

### 10. CLIMATE & WEATHER
- **Riigi Ilmateenistus / Keskkonnaagentuur (ilmateenistus.ee):** climate normals (kliimanormid) for air temperature and precipitation (incl. max 24 h), plus station observations (temperature, precipitation, wind, snow cover, growing season); maps/tables per station with downloadable underlying data.
- **European gridded fill-ins covering Estonia:**
  - **E-OBS (Copernicus/ECA&D):** daily gridded TG/TN/TX, precipitation, sea-level pressure, humidity, wind, global radiation; **0.1° and 0.25°**; 1950–present; NetCDF-4. **Non-commercial research/education only.**
  - **ERA5 / ERA5-Land (Copernicus C3S):** ERA5 ~31 km hourly (1940–present); **ERA5-Land ~9 km / 0.1°** hourly (1950–present) — temperature, precipitation, soil moisture, snow, wind; free/open via Climate Data Store.
- **Ranked #1 (climate):** Estonian station normals for point calibration; **ERA5-Land (9 km)** for consistent gridded per-location climate; E-OBS for daily observed extremes (mind the non-commercial licence).

### 11. PROTECTED AREAS & ADMINISTRATIVE
- **Kaitsealad / protected areas & Natura 2000 (EELIS):** protected-area polygons, Natura 2000 (SCI/SPA), hoiualad, püsielupaigad — EELIS GeoServer WFS/WMS; also kaitsealad.ee. Category I/II sensitive layers restricted (agreement + ID-card auth via KRATT).
- **Administrative (EHAK) — Maa-amet:** maakond, omavalitsus, asustusüksus polygons linked to the Statistikaamet EHAK classifier; WMS+WFS via kataster.ee (nightly updates); overview shapefiles + Eesti lõikemask (coastline mask) as JSON.
- **Cadastre (katastri kaart) — Maa-amet:** all parcel polygons + points; attributes include katastritunnus, EHAK code, county/municipality/settlement, address, area, land-use categories (sihtotstarve), kõlvik shares, measurement/formation method, ownership form, taxation value. WMS+WFS at kataster.ee/avaandmed/kaarditeenused (from 8 Apr 2024), updated within 1–2 days; bulk by municipality (SHP/DGN) at 1 Jan snapshot. **Land-use restrictions (kitsendused) only via WMS** (vectors not redistributable; I/II category objects hidden).
- **Ranked #1 (protected/admin):** EELIS for conservation status per location; kataster.ee cadastre + EHAK for parcels and administrative context.

### 12. BASE TOPOGRAPHY & IMAGERY
- **ETAK (Eesti topograafia andmekogu):** full national topographic database — buildings, structures, land cover (kõlvikud), landforms (pinnavormid), water network, place names — 39 WFS layers, daily updates; source of Eesti põhikaart 1:10 000, Baaskaart, Topo250.
- **Orthophotos (ortofoto) — Maa-amet:** topographic orthos at **20–40 cm GSD** cover the whole country; dense-settlement orthos at **10–16 cm** (some city blocks 3.2–5 cm with Leica CityMapper-2). Channels: RGB, false-colour NRG, and forestry CIR/NGR (near-IR for vegetation / conifer-broadleaf / wet-area discrimination). A whole-country "lina" mosaic fuses the latest orthos of varying resolution; annual spring + summer forestry campaigns. WMS + bulk by map sheet (wget/BAT for mass download).
- **Base maps:** Eesti põhikaart (colour/greyscale/relief-shaded), Baaskaart (generalised ETAK), Topo250, plus historical Soviet & tsarist topo maps (1:10 000–1:1,000,000) via kaart.maaamet.ee/wms/ajalooline.
- **Ranked #1 (base/imagery):** ETAK + latest orthophoto mosaic as visual/positional ground truth; CIR/NGR orthos to refine vegetation typing.

## Recommendations (staged)

**Stage 1 — Physical skeleton (terrain + surface + soils).** Pull Maa-amet **1 m DTM, 1 m nDSM, 4 m CHM**, **ETAK** (all 39 layers, esp. kõlvikud + hydrography), and **Mullastikukaart 1:10 000**. These four give bare-earth terrain, object/canopy heights, wall-to-wall land cover + water geometry, and soils for essentially all rural Estonia. All via WFS/WCS + bulk, L-EST97/EH2000, CC BY 4.0. *Benchmark to advance:* full national tile coverage ingested and consistently reprojected.

**Stage 2 — Living cover (forest, habitats, species).** Add **Metsaregister eraldis** (+ eraldis_element, metsakasvukohatüüp) via the metsaregister WFS; **EELIS** habitat, PLK semi-natural community, and mire-inventory layers via the eelis GeoServer; and **GBIF/eElurikkus** occurrence downloads to populate species per biotope. Where Metsaregister is stale, synthesise stand structure from CHM + Sentinel HRL/tree-genus products. *Benchmark:* every ETAK forest/grassland/mire polygon carries a stand or habitat/site-type attribute.

**Stage 3 — Subsurface, climate, water depth.** Add **EGT 1:50 000 pinnakate + WCS 50 m Quaternary-thickness/bedrock-relief grids** (fall back to 1:400 000 outside the 55-sheet footprint), **ERA5-Land 9 km** climate fields (+ Estonian station normals), and **TLÜ lake bathymetry / EMODnet marine DTM** where available; model remaining lake depths from morphometry. *Benchmark:* a per-location climate vector and a subsurface/Quaternary attribute everywhere.

**Stage 4 — Context & QA.** Add cadastre + EHAK (kataster.ee), protected areas/Natura (EELIS), and the latest orthophoto mosaic + CIR for visual QA and vegetation refinement; cross-check land cover against ESA WorldCover 10 m / Copernicus HRL.

**Thresholds that change the plan:**
- If you need *current* wall-to-wall forest (not sample or stale standwise), invest in your own CHM + Sentinel classification rather than relying on Metsaregister.
- If lake depth matters physically, commission or await TLÜ bathymetric monitoring for target lakes — historical charts (1906–2019) may misrepresent current basins.
- If a location falls in the geology gap (outside ~55 sheets), rely on the soil map's peat/texture info + 1:400 000 geology rather than the 1:50 000 base map.

## Caveats
- **Soil map is 1:10 000 vector, not a 1 m raster**, and excludes cities/water/islets — a per-metre "soil at this pixel" requires interpolating polygon attributes onto the DTM grid, and urban gaps need geology as substitute.
- **LiDAR density is low nationwide** (~0.15–0.45 pt/m² older; ~0.8–2.1 pt/m² newer) except low-altitude cities (18 pt/m²) — fine understory 3D reconstruction is only feasible in madallend-covered urban areas.
- **DTM vertical RMSE is not officially published** as a single number — only QA methodology (control-point std. dev. ≤3 cm); do not quote a specific RMSE without primary confirmation.
- **Standwise forest inventory currency:** private-forest stands can be >20 yr old or absent; SMI is a statistical sample (~3,000 plots + 4,000 model trees/yr), not a map.
- **Geological base-map coverage is incomplete** (~55–60 of ~100 sheets, much undigitised); WCS grids are interpolated and may diverge from real subsurface.
- **Bathymetry is sparse** — digital depth data exists for only the largest lakes plus a few hundred mostly-historical small-lake charts; most water depths must be modelled.
- **Access restrictions:** category I/II protected species and habitat locations are withheld from public EELIS/Maa-amet services; kitsendused vectors are WMS-only.
- **Licence divergence:** E-OBS is non-commercial-only (problematic for a commercial game) — prefer open ERA5-Land for gridded climate. Most core layers are CC BY 4.0-equivalent and safe for commercial use with attribution.
- **WFS 5000-object cap** on all Maa-amet/EELIS services requires paged downloads.
- **Mire decline statistics** (642,200 → 232,900 ha; ~240,000 ha remaining) are widely cited from Paal & Leibak but not independently verified here; the robust, confirmed statement is that ~22% of Estonia is peat-covered and roughly three-quarters of it is artificially drained.
