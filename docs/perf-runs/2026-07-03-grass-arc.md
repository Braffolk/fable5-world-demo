World-wide default-on (1b37cfd) delivered coverage but: fps 35-40 dropping to 15-20 after
minutes (sustained GPU peg → thermal downclock, the known DVFS behavior), and the 4-variant
DAG checkerboard is unacceptable. USER: "some games achieve billions of grass blades easily

pipeline at range.

### Why the patch-DAG lane fails (measured)
- Near band: +4.66M tris at an eye pose (50k tris/patch × ~30m of L0) through the FULL
  election — the base world already rides the 60fps edge; there was never 4-8M tris of
  headroom. The plan §1.3's "fragments dominate, setup is bounded" budget was optimistic.
- 508k instances re-scanned by kSeedRoots every frame (97% early-out, still ~0.5M threads).
- The DAG coarse levels are still GEOMETRY — the far field pays triangles for what the
  splat should carry.
- Checkerboard: 4 variants have 4 independent error ladders → different cut levels at equal
  range; adjacent patches alternate fine-green/wide-straw.

### THE RETHINK (= the plan's own §7 S7 escalation, now the main lane)
GoT-class factoring: **procedural near-band blades generated in compute** (third work-queue
class beside qVoxRaster — the voxel queue proves the raster accepts a second emit class):
- 0-~40m ONLY: blades generated per frame from pcg(worldCell) + the density fields — zero
  stored geometry, zero instances, count exactly matched to screen coverage, screen-clamped
  ribbon widths (≥1px law by construction). No patches ⇒ no grid, no checkerboard, no edges.
- ~40-120m: sparse stored tufts (cheap, few tris) OR generated coarse ribbons — decide by
  measurement.
- Beyond: the terrain splat (already palette-matched + sheen) carries the look — ZERO tris.
- The patch-DAG lane (GrassPatch + aggregate DAG) is DEMOTED to reference/fallback.
