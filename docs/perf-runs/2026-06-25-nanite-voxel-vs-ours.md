# Nanite voxel-foliage vs ours — cost-gap study + red-team verdict (2026-06-25)

Red-team gate over the research→synthesis cycle comparing UE5 Nanite's voxel-foliage
raster against our TSL/WGSL software voxel raster (Apple M-series TBDR, Chrome/Dawn→Metal),
forest of ~200k trees. Every file:line below was re-verified against source this session
unless tagged `[unverified-this-session]`.

---

## 1. PREMISE-AUDIT VERDICT — the comparison is NOT like-for-like, but subtracting the
## confounds RELOCATES the gap onto a large PORTABLE structural residual; it does not dissolve it.

The headline "UE 2-4ms on tens-of-thousands of trees vs ours >10x worse on ~200k" is unfair on
four axes simultaneously. Listed ONLY to subtract them and expose the residual:

- **(A) HARDWARE (non-portable, ~constant).** Desktop RTX-class with a 64-bit image-atomic
  visbuffer (`ImageInterlockedMaxUInt64`), fixed-function rasterizer, ROP/ZCull early-Z, R64.
  We have a 32-bit-atomic software scatter on a TBDR GPU in a browser. WGSL has no 64-bit atomic
  and no conservative-depth early-Z (writing `@builtin(frag_depth)` disables early-Z). Real, but
  a **per-fragment constant factor, not the order of magnitude.**
- **(B) TEMPORAL / RESOLUTION (UNVERIFIED, quality-gated).** The "2-4ms" is plausibly a TSR
  internal-resolution, temporally-amortized figure; ours is native retina 2268×1473 full-rate
  (canonical p0.95 = 43.8ms). BUT the supplied UE shaders (`AutoVoxel/RasterizeBricks/ScatterBricks/
  TileBricks.usf`) are **per-frame with NO history buffer** — the TSR claim is about an unseen
  integration layer. Forbidden as a portable fix anyway: TSR/TAA upscale ghosts sub-pixel moving
  foliage, violating the zero-visible-quality-loss-to-a-moving-viewer constraint.
- **(C) WORKLOAD-MIX (this is the residual in disguise — DO NOT subtract).** UE voxels are an
  error-chosen LOD *alternative* that engages only where a voxel is ~pixel-sized (the far slice);
  near crowns stay triangle-Nanite. So "2-4ms voxel" is a thin far slice. BUT that thinness is
  *generated by* the pixel-size-LOD law — which is exactly the structural residual to hunt.
  Counting it as a separate confound double-counts away the cause.
- **(D) METRIC / TREE COUNT.** ~4-10x of the raw headline is simply 200k vs tens-of-thousands of
  trees and belongs to per-tree normalization; and the true cost may sit upstream in cull
  over-emission, not the raster shader.

**Apparent-10x accounting (equal-image basis, confounds subtracted not excused):**
- ~2-3x non-portable HW (64-bit atomic + ROP early-Z + TBDR-in-browser) — constant, NOT the 10x.
- ~1-1.5x temporal/resolution — UNVERIFIED and quality-forbidden; do not bank.
- **≥4-5x PORTABLE structural residual in WORK EMITTED — the order of magnitude lives here.**

The user directive is upheld: **parameterisation/tuning/hardware cannot explain 10x.** The residual
is structural and portable. The HW gap is a tax on each fragment; the portable gap is the **NUMBER**
of fragments, and number is where 10x lives.

far-dominated forest:

## 3. RED-TEAM VERDICT ON THE SYNTHESIS

**The synthesis is largely SOUND. The named biggest win survives.** I confirm the structural
diagnosis and the ranking. Corrections and sharpenings below.

### 3.1 Load-bearing claims VERIFIED against source this session
- ✅ Rank-1 rests on a real bug: `dArr[db]=0` / `childCount=0` at GeometryRegistry.ts:1114/1119,
  with one `dagLinks` root per cluster (`:1120`). The selector multiplies pOwn by ownError, so
  pOwn≡0 ≤ τ ALWAYS and there is nothing to descend to. **Genuine degenerate single-level DAG.**
- ✅ Rank-2 is real: the Phase-B loop `loopU(brickLocal, area=bbW·bbH, …)` elects every footprint
  pixel (NaniteVoxelRaster.ts:739-746); `BRICK_OCC` is referenced **zero** times in the raster file.
- ✅ Rank-3 is real: kVoxFanout appends `uv2(instId, ci)` per entry (NaniteCull.ts:503), no dedup.
- ✅ Rank-4 is real: `atomicAdd(atomicBuf[WRITE_CTR],1)` at :761 has no build gate.

### 3.2 LOAD-BEARING ERROR the critique caught (and I re-confirm) — keep it dead
The **original** RAST-3 / HW-1 framing ("2-4 global atomics **per elected pixel**", "~2-3x
per-fragment HW tax") was **WRONG**. Source: lines 756-762 nest `atomicStore(visBV)` and
`atomicAdd(WRITE_CTR)` **inside the double-`If` win branch**. The common-path cost per footprint
pixel is **one relaxed load + a conditional `atomicMax`**; the second store fires only on a depth
WIN (rare). The corrected residual (~1.1-1.2x, mostly self-inflicted/win-only) is right. **Anyone
re-opening "fix the atomic" as a 2-3x lever is chasing a refuted number.**

### 3.3 Sharpening: the visBV split is forced by ~60 bits, not "30-bit id"
Per-pixel winner identity = depth(24) + brick-within-cluster id8 (in the key) + qVoxRaster item
index (≤28 bits, in visBV) ≈ **60 bits** total (line 22 comment: "election WORD is
`depthKey24(24) | id8`"; `voxId = VOX_BIT | payload`, payload < QVOX_CAP < 1<<28, line 452/80).
So rank-5's single-atomic collapse needs BOTH (a) the item index shrunk via LOD/dedup AND (b)
brick-id folded into the dispatch entry — **more contingent than stated.** Constant factor; correctly
de-prioritised.

### 3.4 SURFACED for the user's call — a structural question the portfolio does NOT list as a lever
GO-UP-A-LEVEL: UE's voxels cover only the **far** slice (near crowns are triangle-Nanite). Our
voxel band is `lodDist` 35→2000m. **Distance-LOD (rank-1) shrinks the FAR field but does nothing
for the NEAR band** — near voxels stay fine by design. If a meaningful fraction of our voxel cost
is the near/mid band that UE renders as triangles (not voxels at all), then **whether the 35m near
bound should be pushed out (voxels engage later, triangles cover nearer)** is a separate structural
divergence not captured by rank-1/2/3. This sits between "parameter" (the 35m constant) and
"structure" (the near=triangle / far=voxel split). It must be MEASURED (far-field fraction, open
Q in §5) before rank-1's realized gain can be trusted, and it may be an independent lever. **Not
silently parked — flagged for decision.**

### 3.5 Ranking is frame-mix-dependent (honest caveat on the biggest win)
Rank-1's gain scales with the **far-field fraction** of the frame; a near-dominated framing
collapses it toward 1x. If `?voxdither` is default-ON (open Q), rank-2's elected-atomic win is
partly pre-captured and its residual is loop-iteration + exact-silhouette + the opaque-air quality
fix. So rank-1 vs rank-2 ordering can invert by framing — **the synthesis's "both must land
together to hit 2-4ms" is the correct, defensible position** and I do not override it.

## 4. RANKED PORTFOLIO (file:line on both sides, TSL/WGSL sketches)

### Rank 1 — Distance-LOD voxel MIP pyramid + a REAL multi-level voxel DAG (BIGGEST PORTABLE WIN)
**Mechanism.** Replace the always-cut ownError=0/childCount=0 DAG. The runtime cull BFS selector
(`NaniteCull.ts:809,817` `[unverified-this-session]`, but independently confirmed by memory
`voxel-single-resolution-no-dag-lod`) is already correct and merely **starved of multi-level data**.
The bug lives one level up in the **build + DAG-authoring**, not the shader.
**Est.** ~2-5x far-field brick+fragment reduction (3-8x upper envelope, far-fraction-gated).
DERIVED from UE's log2 law over the 35-2000m band; **not measured this session.**
**Feasibility.** Fully portable — no 64-bit atomic, no wave op, no hot-path storage buffer (coarse
levels ADD a few persistent bricks but FAR fewer rastered). Build-time work only.
**Sketch.** `VoxelizeCrown.ts`: after the fine grid, emit log2 octave levels by 2× downsample,
child-occupied⇒parent-occupied (OR-merge sub-bricks into parent occupancy words); author coarser
**clusters** (the DAG operates at cluster granularity — coarse levels need fewer/bigger-brick
clusters, not just merged masks). `GeometryRegistry.ts:1114-1120`: set per-level `ownError` =
world voxel-extent / DistanceFactor (so pOwn crosses τ at the pixel-size threshold), `childCount>0`,
and real `dagLinks` child offsets. No change to the selector math.
**Falsifiable test.** Per-frame counter of emitted qVoxRaster items (the fanout append site,
NaniteCull.ts:503) binned by distance band: BEFORE flat in distance; AFTER falls ~quadratically.
Visual gate (memory `verify-user-observable-output`): far canopy visibly coarsens with NO popping
to a moving viewer. Anchor with a WebGPU-Inspector whole-frame gpuWall capture.
**UE ref.** `AutoVoxel.usf:16-34`, `Brick.ush:48,51-62,76`.

### Rank 2 — Occupancy-aware raster: read the baked mask, emit the occupied silhouette
**Mechanism.** Phase B paints the full AABB at one front-slab depth; the baked 64-bit occupancy
(`BRICK_OCC_LO/HI`, words 0/1) is read 0× at raster. Read it and either (a) derive a tight
occupied-AABB to shrink the rect, or (b) per-pixel DDA-march the two u32 words and discard ray-miss.
**Est.** ~1.5-3x fewer elected fragments per brick (revised DOWN from 3-6x: a 4-deep brick's
silhouette covers ~50-70%, not 1/density; lower end if `?voxdither` default-on). Doubles as a
**correctness fix** (kills opaque-air, gives per-pixel depth that improves the existing cross-brick
relaxed-load occlusion gate).
**Feasibility.** Fully portable — two-word bit tests, no u64 atomic, no wave op, no ROP. Caveat:
the point-splat sub-variant needs ~pixel-sized voxels (couples to rank-1); the **DDA-march (a) is
the safe standalone route.**
**Sketch.** Phase A (`:556-560` `[unverified-this-session]`): also load words 0/1 per brick into
shared arrays. Phase B (`:739-746`): per footprint pixel reconstruct the voxel-space ray, DDA-step
the 4³ cells, bit-test `BRICK_OCC`; first hit → compute that voxel's depth → existing election
(`:755-759`); ray clears all cells → skip (no atomic).
**Test.** elected-pixels per brick BEFORE/AFTER (~1.5-3x drop); a sparse conifer brick renders
see-through; `BRICK_OCC` now appears in the compiled raster WGSL.
**UE ref.** `ScatterBricks.usf:44-73`, `RasterizeBricks.usf:238,256-257`, `Voxel.ush:621-744`.

### Rank 3 — Remove the unconditional `atomicAdd(WRITE_CTR)` (free, do immediately)
Gate line 761 behind the existing debug-flag mechanism (same pattern as `?voxoccl` at `:261`). Fires
on WINS only → contention lower than headlined (~1.01-1.05x), but **free and zero-risk.** Output
byte-identical. UE ref `NaniteWritePixel.ush:67-77`.

### Rank 4 — Cross-instance/screen dedup of the fanned set (RESIDUAL after rank-1; do NOT add to it)
~1.2-1.5x residual once far trees coarsen. NEEDS-TRANSLATION: a full UE depth-buffer HashTableAdd
fights our offline-baked persistent-buffer model; a runtime world-space hash IS expressible but
costs one of the ~10 storage-buffer slots + atomics (the 11th regressed before). **Sequence AFTER
rank-1; only pursue if a residual overlap multiplier is still measured.** Sketch: atomicCAS-insert
a quantized world-voxel-center key before the append at NaniteCull.ts:500-505. UE ref
`AutoVoxel.usf:44-94`.

### Rank 5 — Collapse the 2-buffer split into one 32-bit atomicMax (CONTINGENT, constant-factor)
~1.1-1.2x. Gated on shrinking per-pixel identity to ≤8 bits — needs BOTH the item index shrunk
(rank-1/4) AND brick-id carried by the dispatch entry (see §3.3, ~60 bits today). UE's u64
`ImageInterlockedMaxUInt64` is **infeasible** in WGSL; the 32-bit collapse is the re-derivation.
Do NOT attempt standalone. UE ref `ScatterBricks.usf:10-23`.

### Rank 6 — Shrink the 9-word (36B) brick record toward ~16B indirection (cleanup-tier)
~1.0-1.2x live read bandwidth, ~2x persistent RAM/L2 (matters vs the ~2GB host Uint32Array cap, not
per-frame ms). Derive center+half from a packed grid coord + cellSize (UE derives CubeSize from the
key); drop unused spread/albedo from the default path; **keep occupancy (rank-2 reads it).** Low
value to sequence before rank-1. UE ref `Brick.ush:13-17,51-77`.

## 5. KILLED IDEAS (do not re-litigate)
- **Sort-middle tile binning (UE TileBricks)** — INFEASIBLE (needs `WaveInterlockedAdd` list alloc;
  no subgroup ops in TSL r184) **AND already measured net-losing and REMOVED** in-setup
  (NaniteVoxelRaster.ts:1-28, commit eecf046). The setup-floor argument applies to screen-tile bins
  too. Wrong target.
- **HW ROP early-Z / SV_DepthLessEqual** — INFEASIBLE: WebGPU has no conservative-depth early-Z;
  writing `frag_depth` disables early-Z. Much of the cost ascribed here is our software
  occlusion-pyramid EMULATION, which shrinks once emission is fixed — not a fixed HW constant.
- **Wave/subgroup intrinsics as a perf lever** — MIRAGE: the wave-dependent UE code (TileBricks list
  alloc, AutoVoxel hash) is in paths we DON'T run. ~1.0x contribution today.
- **Thread-per-voxel dispatch as an independent win** — MISATTRIBUTED: the Phase-A barrier + idle
  lanes + shared-mem roundtrip are overhead to MANAGE the solid-bbox area variance; they dissolve
  automatically when rank-2 lands. Not separately actionable.
- **Per-pixel cross-brick early-Z as a standalone build** — largely already present (the relaxed-load
  gate at :755 makes an occluded pixel pay only one load) + the rest is a consequence of rank-2.
- **Improving the indirect-dispatch SHAPE** — 0x: the split2D 2D-split indirect dispatch
  (DISPATCH_ROW=65535) is already correct; the launched-WG count is too large because of upstream
  no-LOD/no-dedup, not the dispatch shape.
- **Terminating on "it's just HW / TSR / parameters"** — BANNED and refuted: HW is a ~2-3x constant,
  TSR is unverified + quality-forbidden. Neither explains 10x.

## 6. OPEN QUESTIONS (gate the study; measure before committing)
1. **Far-field FRACTION of a representative 200k-tree frame.** Rank-1's realized gain (2-5x vs 3-8x)
   scales directly with it; near-dominated framing collapses the win. Measure emitted-bricks per
   distance band first. **This also answers §3.4** (is the near band, which UE renders as triangles,
   a large slice of our voxel cost — an independent structural lever?).
2. **Is `?voxdither` default-ON in the canonical config?** If yes, rank-2's elected-atomic win is
   partly pre-captured; its residual shifts toward loop-iteration + exact-silhouette + the quality fix.
3. **Does rank-4 dedup survive the ~10-storage-buffer ceiling**, and is its post-rank-1 residual
   (~1.2-1.5x) worth a slot that previously regressed at #11? A/B only AFTER rank-1.
4. **No isolated voxel-pass ms exists in this study** — all numbers are op-count/work-emitted ratios.
   A WebGPU-Inspector whole-frame gpuWall capture (memory `nanite-perf-capture-method`: trust
   gpuWall, not per-pass timestamps) is required to anchor every gain and confirm rank-1 dominates.
5. **Can a coarser far voxel level hold the zero-visible-quality-loss-to-a-moving-viewer bar** without
   LOD-transition popping? Gate on the user-observable visual (does far visibly coarsen smoothly?),
   not an internal correctness report.
6. **`[unverified-this-session]`** NaniteCull.ts:809/817 selector math and NaniteVoxelRaster.ts:556-560
   Phase-A reads — trusted via memory + surrounding code, not re-read here. Confirm before implementing
   rank-1/2.

## 7. BOTTOM LINE
The 10x is real and **structural**, not tuning/HW. After honestly subtracting ~2-3x non-portable HW
and an unverified ~1-1.5x temporal factor, a **≥4-5x portable residual in WORK EMITTED** remains,
owned by three compounding over-emissions confirmed in our source: **no distance-LOD (the dominant
lever)**, **solid-bbox fill**, and **no dedup**. The biggest portable win is **Rank-1 (distance-LOD
voxel MIP pyramid + real multi-level DAG)** — gated on the far-field fraction and paired with Rank-2
(occupancy-march). Surface to the user the §3.4 question (near-band-voxelized-at-all) before
committing, and anchor every gain with a gpuWall capture (open Q4).
