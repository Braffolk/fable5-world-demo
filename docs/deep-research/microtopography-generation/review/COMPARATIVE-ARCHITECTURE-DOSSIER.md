# Comparative Architecture Decision Dossier

**Status:** architecture decision for the replacement microtopography spec, 2026-07-13
**Scope:** cook-side source correction and synthesis only. The accepted packed-height transport remains the output boundary. This document does not authorize implementation.

## 1. Decision

Adopt an **evidence-gated typed hybrid surface architecture**.

This is one architecture, not a menu of national backbones:

1. **Typed deterministic reconstruction owns every structure for which direct evidence exists.** It converts the fallible 1 m DTM plus ETAK, water, object, and confidence evidence into a corrected structural authority. A generative model may not move a mapped shore, invent relief on open water, or decide whether a known escarpment exists.
2. **A deterministic regime and support-domain graph owns eligibility, transitions, uncertainty, and abstention.** It represents overlapping substrate, soil, hydrology, exposure, vegetation, management, disturbance, observation, and heightfield-capability states. A broad `forest`, `field`, `rock`, or `bog` label never selects final geometry by itself.
3. **Unresolved morphology is generated by a validated specialist for each physical regime.** Contextual exemplar transfer, conditional geostatistical simulation, calibrated process/event simulation, learned regression, GANs, pixel-space diffusion, and hybrids are candidate implementations behind the same specialist contract. No family is the implicit fallback. A regime's production owner is the winner of a preregistered, target-supported bake-off for that regime.
4. **A deterministic constrained composer owns the final surface.** It reconciles specialist geometry with the corrected authority, re-applies forbidden-domain and structural constraints, mediates physical transitions, and produces one canonical absolute 0.0625 m master surface.
5. **The existing cook owns storage hierarchy and publication.** LOD -2 is canonicalized through the real encoder/decoder, LOD -1 and corrected LOD 0 are derived from decoded accepted children, affected LOD 1-4 ancestors are rederived, and ordinary packed height is published. The browser only streams and samples it.

The architecture deliberately rejects both initial reports' universal answer. `r1.md` promotes pixel-space diffusion before a terrain prior, real observation model, or centimeter corpus exists. `r2.md` promotes deterministic sparse/process synthesis beyond the regimes those methods demonstrate. Both contain useful components; neither establishes a national owner.

The result is decisive in the point that matters: **there will be no monolithic learned backbone, no deterministic procedural fallback, and no decorative universal residual.** There will be one typed reconstruction/composition pipeline and an explicit release registry of proven regime specialists. Because the product requirement is credible added detail across Estonia, an unsupported common regime blocks the national claim. Returning only the corrected base is safe behavior, not completion of that requirement.

## 2. Premises That Control The Decision

### 2.1 The visual objective is distributional realism, not consistency theater

The objective is beautiful, realistic, non-repeating geometric variance conditioned by the material and process that formed it. Quantization accuracy, seams, hierarchy consistency, and determinism are release constraints. They are not a proxy for beauty.

The 1 m DTM is a fallible observation. Exact DDNM-style projection is valid only for a known linear observation operator. Maa-amet's raster includes classification, interpolation, missing support, water behavior, vegetation leakage, and other errors; it is not known to be a block average of a latent 0.0625 m surface. Projecting every fine result back to every raw 1 m value would preserve diagnosed defects by design. The corrected structural authority, not the raw raster, is the hierarchy target.

### 2.2 No audited paper solves the requested transformation

No reviewed source demonstrates realistic Estonia-wide `1 m -> 0.0625 m` ground synthesis. The closest learned terrain systems operate at approximately 2-90 m outputs, synthetic degradations, or different remote-sensing tasks. The deterministic methods solve restricted exemplar, hydraulic, event, or authored-terrain problems. Method ideas transfer; weights, material distributions, accuracy, and national claims do not.

The local target audit found:

- zero production-paired Estonia target sites;
- zero open target-scale Estonia height surfaces;
- only 309.1387 m2 of ready open centimeter-scale peat exemplars in 68 small foreign plots;
- no ready target-scale height exemplar for nine of ten audited broad physical release regimes.

That evidence rules out predeclaring any universal generator. It also makes acquisition a production dependency, not an optional polish pass.

### 2.3 Packed rungs are representation levels, not model stages

The physical contract remains:

| Rung | Texel | Chunk footprint | Payload |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| LOD 0 | 1 m | 2,048 m | `2049 x 2049` |
| LOD -1 | 0.25 m | 512 m | `2049 x 2049` |
| LOD -2 | 0.0625 m | 128 m | `2049 x 2049` |

This does not prove that two independent x4 networks are statistically correct. A specialist may use a direct finest-field model, a multiscale model, more internal scales, an event domain, or a process domain. It still returns one finest accepted surface, from which stored parents are derived. LOD 0 is not redefined as 128 m, and LOD -1/-2 are not generated independently.

### 2.4 A 6.25 cm sample pitch is not a promise of 6.25 cm objects

The nominal two-sample Nyquist wavelength is 12.5 cm. Recognizable irregular stones, cracks, clods, lips, and roots generally need more support and must survive triangulation, quantization, normal construction, and LOD transition. Millimeter grains, leaf litter, grass blades, and optical texture are not valid height claims merely because a 6.25 cm grid exists.

### 2.5 The frozen heightfield has a hard topological limit

One elevation per horizontal coordinate cannot represent a vertical wall, undercut, cave, overhang, detached rock, or root plate. The fine heightfield may improve the single-valued cliff top, bank shoulder, talus, ledges, and slopes. It cannot make the Suur Taevaskoda wall topologically match the reference. Non-heightfield regions must be identified and the fine-height claim must abstain there. A separate structural representation is a different project.

## 3. Comparative Method Judgment

The ratings below concern ownership in this project, not whether a method is valuable in general.

| Family | Direct evidence and credible strength | Binding failure for national ownership | Assigned role |
|---|---|---|---|
| typed deterministic reconstruction | Direct vectors and sensor disagreement can define water, shore, escarpment, road, ditch, quarry, object contamination, and other structural constraints. It is auditable and conservative. | It cannot invent the unresolved national centimeter distribution, and overbroad cleanup can destroy real depressions, boulders, or cliffs. | **Mandatory owner of directly evidenced source repair and hard structure.** Learned assistance is limited to ambiguous detection with calibrated abstention. |
| sparse paired dictionary | Guérin et al. 2016 transfers high-resolution atoms from paired low/high exemplars and preserves measured local morphology better than generic noise when target and exemplar are similar. | The paper requires similar exemplars and does not guarantee geomorphological or fine-scale coherence. Sparse/local matching can repeat, transition poorly, and lacks a causal national inference. | Serious specialist challenger for target-rich, locally similar regimes; never a national fallback. |
| contextual/multilayer exemplar | Argudo et al. 2017 jointly matches elevation, slope/orientation, drainage, vegetation, and soil-like layers, improving context over nearest-patch transfer. | Dictionary coverage still controls the ceiling; structured features may fail to join, unseen physical combinations are unsupported, and centimeter Estonia transfer is unproven. | Preferred nonparametric challenger where target corpus variety and contextual layers are adequate. |
| calibrated process/event | Hydraulic erosion, pit-mound event theory, tillage operations, fracture/solution processes, and peat organization can preserve causal shape, topology, orientation, and long-range structure within their valid domains. | Every audited process family is partial. Parameters and event distributions are not Estonia constants; wrong eligibility produces conspicuously wrong terrain. Some processes require watersheds, mire units, stands, or parcels larger than inference windows. | First-class owner candidate for phenomena with known cause, state, domain, and target calibration. It generates the causal form before storage cropping. |
| conditional geostatistical simulation | Variograms, cross-covariance, anisotropy, marked-point processes, and conditional random fields can reproduce calibrated stationary or locally stationary distributions with transparent uncertainty and low cook cost. | Marginal spectra/variograms do not specify recognizable objects, topology, event history, or multimodal contextual layouts. A Gaussian roughness field readily becomes the forbidden noise result. | Challenger only for residual components whose target evidence supports the assumed statistics; not for shores, channels, cracks, pits/mounds, furrows, or other typed forms by itself. |
| learned deterministic regressor | Efficient multimodal fusion can infer a conditional mean, assist artifact classification, and reconstruct structures correlated with DTM/imagery. Argudo et al. 2018 establishes optical benefit at 15 m to 2 m. | L1/L2 regression smooths one-to-many details. Existing work stops at meter scale and reports canopy, shadow, and small-feature failures. Confidence emitted by the same network is not automatically calibrated. | Strong challenger for ambiguous source correction and predictable structure; insufficient default owner for unresolved variance. |
| conditional GAN | GATA demonstrates x4 terrain theme amplification and learned local variation at its scale. GAN inference is cheaper than iterative diffusion and can generate sharp stochastic detail. | No released target data/checkpoint solves this domain. Mode collapse, training instability, repetition, and weak calibrated coverage are material risks; theme/style is not physical conditioning. | High-throughput stochastic specialist challenger where real targets exist. It wins only on blind distribution coverage and tails, not sharpness alone. |
| pixel-space conditional diffusion | Pixel-space generation keeps metric height explicit and is a plausible high-ceiling model for multimodal unresolved morphology. Shared global noise and overlap consensus can support deterministic large output. | DDNM supplies consistency, not a terrain prior. MultiDiffusion supplies pixel consensus, not slope/drainage continuity. Published terrain diffusion priors are macro-scale. Iterative national inference is expensive, and current target data are radically insufficient. | Highest-ceiling learned specialist challenger for well-supported regimes. It does not own source repair or become a national default before evidence and cost gates. |
| hybrid specialists | Process/exemplar structure can preserve topology and event shape while a learned or statistical residual supplies target-calibrated within-form variability. Deterministic reconciliation can enforce hard structure afterward. | Hybrids can become an unprincipled stack whose parts duplicate bands, fight constraints, or hide unsupported transfer. | **Expected production form for many regimes**, but every component needs explicit scale/domain ownership, ablation, and a removal gate. |

### 3.1 Why the learned-backbone proposal loses

A unified conditional model is attractive because the physical state is high-dimensional. That does not show it can extrapolate through combinations absent from training. A large model can hide unsupported combinations behind plausible averages, memorize geography, ignore coarse covariates, emboss orthophoto albedo, or overexpress rare dramatic terrain. The evidence supports learned models as specialists, not as an evidence substitute.

### 3.2 Why the deterministic-backbone proposal loses

Determinism is required for the bake, not for the morphology family. A coordinate-seeded stochastic model can be deterministic in production. Sparse dictionaries and hand/process models do not span all Estonia, and a growing catalog of uncalibrated functions recreates the rejected result at higher complexity: generic bumps, visible patterns, hard class transitions, and systemic knobs.

### 3.3 Why the hybrid architecture wins

The target contains different epistemic types:

- **observed structure** should be reconstructed, not hallucinated;
- **process-governed structure** should preserve its causal domain and topology;
- **measured but weakly modeled morphology** is suited to exemplar or learned distribution transfer;
- **calibrated stochastic residuals** may be simulated statistically;
- **unsupported structure** must abstain rather than borrow an unrelated prior.

No single method family handles those types honestly. A typed hybrid assigns each one to the narrowest capable owner while retaining one coherent output surface.

## 4. Exact Component Ownership

### 4.1 Artifact model

The cook must keep these artifacts distinct:

| Artifact | Meaning | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| `raw_observation` | Maa-amet 1 m DTM and source/acquisition state | evidence, never infallible truth |
| `structural_authority` | corrected single-valued ground/bed with typed water, shore, bank, escarpment, corridor, and object constraints | deterministic reconstruction output; ordinary corrected LOD 0 authority |
| `regime_state` | overlapping physical states, confidence, support scale, transition domains, and representation capability | deterministic evidence graph |
| `unresolved_realization` | one recipe-bound sample of only those forms the observations do not determine | selected regime specialists |
| `final_master` | constrained absolute EH2000 surface at the canonical finest grid | deterministic composer output |
| `packed_hierarchy` | browser-equivalent quantized/decoded LOD -2, derived -1, corrected 0, and affected ancestors | existing height cook/release authority |

### 4.2 Conditioning and provenance owner

The input assembler owns registration, dates, hashes, confidence, support scale, and missing-data masks for:

- Maa-amet DTM plus acquisition/interpolation evidence;
- nDSM/CHM disagreement as visibility/object evidence, never ground height;
- full Mullastikukaart mixtures, component shares, layered texture, humus/peat, stoniness, and fertility;
- ETAK water, shoreline, river, escarpment, ditch, road, quarry, wetland, peat field, land cover, and mapped boulder structures;
- EGT 1:50,000 geology/geomorphology with coverage masks and 1:200,000 low-authority fallback;
- dated RGB/CIR orthophoto with water, canopy, object, shadow, snow/season, registration, and temporal-confidence masks;
- forestry, agriculture, management, and disturbance evidence;
- target/exemplar acquisition method, error, surface semantics, license, and transfer class.

Unknown coverage is not a category value. Polygon edges are map boundaries, not relief edges. Every condition has an explicit permitted causal role and effective support.

### 4.3 Structural reconstruction owner

Typed deterministic reconstruction runs before morphology synthesis and owns:

- open-water surface constraints, while preserving the distinct terrain-bed semantic;
- subpixel/world-space shoreline reconstruction instead of the raw 1 m staircase;
- bank side, bank shoulder, and mapped escarpment continuity without blurring them into one ramp;
- ditch/channel connectivity, road/track corridors, quarry/cut/fill structure, and mapped hard features;
- removal of supported building/canopy leakage, spikes, strips, and interpolation artifacts;
- confidence collars and protection of real cliffs, boulders, channels, depressions, and rare landforms.

Ambiguous artifact detection may use a learned regressor/classifier. Its output is advisory unless independently calibrated by class and geography, with an explicit risk-coverage threshold and abstention. It may not silently override direct evidence.

### 4.4 Regime graph owner

The regime graph is a spatial field, not one chunk label. It may activate multiple compatible specialists, such as a till substrate, mineral forest floor, recent windthrow, and local drainage. It owns:

- eligibility predicates and mutually exclusive states;
- mixture weights only where physical mixtures are meaningful;
- connected domains such as watershed, mire unit, forest stand/event field, parcel, shore reach, or outcrop;
- transition distance and the evidence that mediates it;
- target-data release status for each physical band;
- `unsupported`, `research`, `pilot`, or `national` status;
- non-heightfield, below-grid, occluded, and forbidden-residual masks.

No unsupported row inherits a visually similar expert automatically.

### 4.5 Specialist contract

Every specialist consumes a world-aligned physical support domain and returns:

1. absolute or residual metric height on the canonical synthesis grid;
2. a valid/support mask and uncertainty or ensemble evidence;
3. the physical forms and scale range it claims;
4. all long-range state needed to reproduce the form;
5. recipe identity, target registry version, model/process/exemplar hashes, and stochastic-field identity;
6. a failure/abstention result;
7. diagnostics sufficient to test morphology coverage, repetition, and boundary behavior.

It may not read final storage chunk identity as morphology input. Its final output must be invariant to worker count, job order, requested enclosing AOI, neighboring request history, and storage crop on the locked production stack.

### 4.6 Composer owner

The composer does not average arbitrary expert heightmaps. It resolves ownership in this order:

1. preserve corrected direct structure;
2. place validated long-range/process/event forms inside eligible domains;
3. apply a target-supported within-form specialist realization;
4. reconcile overlapping compatible phenomena with their declared physical scale/support;
5. apply confidence-mediated transitions independent of chunk and polygon boundaries;
6. re-apply water, hard-structure, representation, and exclusion constraints;
7. audit drainage, feature continuity, amplitudes, and forbidden relief;
8. emit one absolute finest master.

If two experts claim the same scale and phenomenon, that is a configuration error, not an invitation to blend them.

## 5. Analysis, Determinism, And Packed Hierarchy

### 5.1 One finest authority

The final accepted 0.0625 m surface is canonical. Internal residual names are not assumed to be frequency bands. The implementation spec must define the world-aligned analysis filter, decimation phase, synthesis relationship, aprons, and edge policy. Cross-scale physical forms are generated coherently once, then analyzed into the stored hierarchy.

For an accepted fine region:

1. generate structural correction and specialist morphology over full physical support domains, not output chunks;
2. crop a reconciled LOD -2 master with required synthesis and hierarchy support;
3. quantize, encode, decode, and accept canonical LOD -2 values under the existing checked contract;
4. derive LOD -1 from browser-equivalent decoded LOD -2 children in fixed order;
5. derive corrected LOD 0 from accepted LOD -1 children and publish it through an ordinary corrected format-1 base;
6. rederive every affected LOD 1-4 ancestor and apron dependency;
7. publish the negative-rung overlay against that corrected base.

Known correction must not disappear during `-1 -> 0` morph. Parent consistency targets the corrected authority, never the erroneous raw observation.

### 5.2 Partition-invariant stochastic identity

All stochastic specialists use one counter-based, coordinate-indexed random field bound to the recipe. A diffusion specialist samples crops of the same global initial field; overlapping windows may not initialize the same world point independently. A process specialist derives event identity from the physical domain and stable coordinates, not final chunks.

The production recipe fixes:

- canonical support-domain decomposition and world origin;
- full influencing-domain set for every output crop;
- global stochastic-field algorithm and seed namespace;
- for learned sampling, the exact fused quantity, schedule, overlap lattice, and weights;
- model/checkpoint, inference environment/device class, precision, and deterministic flags;
- source-window/condition hashes and corrected-authority digest;
- analysis/quantization configuration.

Bitwise identity is promised on the locked validated stack. Released packed hashes are the cross-platform authority.

### 5.3 Coverage closure

Every published LOD -1 parent has all 16 LOD -2 children, a corrected LOD 0 parent, and corrected/derived ancestors through the released floor. Fine coverage includes synthesis, normal, morph, and apron support beyond the visible edge. Fine chunks may never exist without their renderable parent chain.

The present release tooling authorizes one LOD -1 parent. A one-parent proof needs no binary, server, renderer, or shader change. Wider coverage later requires generalizing cook/release coverage metadata and auditing to parent-closed rectangles or sets; it does not require new LAC bytes or runtime synthesis.

## 6. Orthophoto Decision

**Fetch dated RGB and CIR orthophoto for the research corpus and likely production. Do not fetch it as height truth.**

Use it for visible shoreline, path, drainage, exposed-material, crop-direction, disturbance, and regime-state evidence. Under canopy, water, shadow, snow, objects, vertical faces, or temporal mismatch, it has low or zero ground authority. A 20-40 cm national image cannot directly supervise the 0.0625-0.25 m height band.

The latest-per-sheet RGB workbook contains 2,111 sheets. An 11-sheet sample projects to approximately 367 GB compressed for national RGB alone, before extraction, history, CIR, masks, and working copies. The Taevaskoda RGB/CIR/NGR archives total about 489.4 MB compressed. Before national acquisition, perform a complete manifest size scan and define versioning, crop/cache, update, and invalidation policy. NGR is optional unless an ablation proves value beyond RGB/CIR.

Orthophoto is enabled for a specialist only if held-out removal, shuffle, registration-perturbation, date-mismatch, canopy/water, and albedo-embossing tests prove geometric benefit.

## 7. National Cost Decision

The zero-runtime-synthesis law makes storage and cook cost part of the architecture.

At a 45,000 km2 land-area envelope, ideal raw sample math is approximately 23.04 TB for LOD -2 plus 1.44 TB for LOD -1 before headers, aprons, indexes, replication, and serving. Under the project's current full-country rectangular chunk-snapped AOI, the cook audit counts 6,576,960 negative-LOD chunks and approximately 55.23 TB raw. Land/coverage masking may reduce that, but requires an explicit parent-closed coverage policy.

The rejected smooth pilot's compression ratio is not admissible evidence for rich generated terrain. Each candidate must be measured through actual quantization, 2D delta coding, deflate, index construction, verification, and serving. Before national approval report p50/p95/p99 by physical regime for:

- wall time and accelerator/CPU hours per km2;
- peak memory, condition I/O, context size, overlap, and model evaluations;
- packed bytes and object count per km2;
- complete national storage, retained-build replication, upload, and serving estimates;
- partial and full recook cost and invalidation fan-out.

High accepted cost is a product decision; hidden cost is an architecture failure. Pixel-space diffusion is not rejected for expense, but cannot win a regime until its measured quality gain justifies its measured cost against GAN, exemplar, process, and hybrid challengers.

## 8. Release Selection And Gates

### 8.1 Required evidence row

Every regime/subtype has one machine-readable release row containing:

1. physical definition, scale ranges, eligible domains, and transition model;
2. direct constraints and forbidden/non-heightfield regions;
3. target sites, surface semantics, effective resolution, modulation transfer, vertical error, visible-ground/support masks, geography, date, license, and hashes;
4. full condition contract and confidence/support scales;
5. geographically/site/campaign-separated train, validation, and untouched tests;
6. fair specialist challengers and frozen compute/parameter allowances;
7. preregistered measured and rendered acceptance rules;
8. partition-invariance, hierarchy, packed entropy, and cook-cost result;
9. failure/abstention behavior and release state.

### 8.2 Data gate

A regime cannot release the 0.25-0.0625 m claim when target transfer or measurement error cannot distinguish its band signal. Nominal grid pitch and point density are insufficient. At least multiple independent acquisition sites/campaigns are required for national status, including Estonia evidence; one foreign site is never enough. Site identity, not random overlapping patches, controls splits.

### 8.3 Structural-repair gate

Compare raw DTM, deterministic typed reconstruction, learned correction, and deterministic-plus-learned ambiguous detection on held-out defects and rare real forms. Measure corrected error, destroyed-landform false positives, uncertainty calibration, and visible hierarchy behavior separately from detail beauty. Any open-water bump after final constraints fails. Any retained visible 1 m shore staircase fails.

### 8.4 Specialist bake-off gate

For each supported regime, compare at least:

- corrected authority with no unresolved detail;
- paired sparse and contextual exemplar where target variety permits;
- conditional geostatistical simulation where its assumptions fit;
- calibrated process/event generation where a causal model exists;
- learned deterministic regressor;
- conditional GAN;
- pixel-space conditional diffusion;
- justified process/exemplar/learned hybrids.

Candidates receive the same target/condition evidence and defensible tuning budget. They are judged per regime; no national average can waive a failed ordinary or rare regime. The rejected LUKE quilt is a failure control, not a competitive baseline.

### 8.5 Visual-beauty gate

Before candidate outputs are viewed, freeze site-separated review paths, camera height, motion, lighting, materials, vegetation state, labels, and reviewer blinding. The decisive gate is blind preference in exact ground-level rendered output, supported by oblique/top-down diagnostics.

A candidate fails if it shows generic noise, uniform bumpiness, sine/patch patterns, repetition, one-meter lattice energy, axis-aligned gradients, stepped shores, water roughness, condition polygon edges, copied optical texture, foreign exemplar identity, implausible material shape/amplitude, or feature discontinuity. Ordinary terrain must be represented at its real variance; dramatic relief may not dominate the prior.

Diagnostics include joint residual-height/gradient/curvature/local-relief distributions, spatial correlations, variograms/spectra, object/event morphology, hydrologic connectivity, tails, descriptor precision and coverage, nearest-training-patch searches, and multi-seed diversity. They diagnose why a render succeeds or fails; they do not overrule the visible result.

### 8.6 Hierarchy and runtime gate

The accepted candidate must survive real encoding and browser-equivalent decode, parent derivation, quantization, edge morphs, and exact runtime sampling. Review includes terrain continuity, material presence, trees/plants, grass grounding, water compatibility, normals, and LOD motion. No synthesis provenance path, shader edit, or runtime algorithm is permitted.

### 8.7 Partition and repetition gate

Recook the same area under different worker counts, order, batches, surrounding requests, and enclosing AOIs. On the locked stack, cropped packed bytes must be identical. Boundary strips must be statistically indistinguishable from interiors for values, gradients, normals, curvature, drainage, and feature continuation. National-scale nearest-neighbor and spectral tests must detect periodic or repeated learned/exemplar motifs.

### 8.8 Owner selection rule

The release system selects exactly one frozen specialist graph per regime/subtype and band claim:

1. discard every candidate that fails data, structural safety, heightfield scope, partition, hierarchy, repetition, or cost-accounting gates;
2. among survivors, require statistically defensible blind rendered preference over corrected-authority-only and non-inferiority on every protected structural/failure measure;
3. rank survivors first by blind ground-level preference, then by held-out morphology precision **and** coverage, then by cross-site robustness and ordinary-terrain calibration;
4. use measured cook/storage cost only as a tie-breaker between quality-equivalent survivors, never to excuse a visibly worse surface;
5. accept a hybrid only when component ablation proves that each part adds a distinct claimed phenomenon or scale without degrading the others;
6. freeze the winning graph, evidence row, recipe, and release extent; a later candidate replaces it only through the same preregistered gate.

If no candidate survives, the row remains `unsupported`. There is no secondary national fallback and no post-hoc vote to select the most attractive failed candidate.

## 9. Rejected Alternatives

- **Exact raw-DTM DDNM projection:** rejected because the real observation operator is not known block averaging and diagnosed raw defects must be corrected. Projection against a corrected parent may remain an internal hierarchy tool.
- **Universal pixel-space diffusion:** rejected as national owner because there is no target corpus, demonstrated centimeter terrain prior, solved structural authority, or acceptable cost evidence. Retained as a high-ceiling specialist challenger.
- **Universal learned regressor:** rejected because conditional means erase the one-to-many variance that defines the task.
- **Universal GAN/theme model:** rejected because sharpness and theme diversity do not establish distribution coverage, material causality, or national support.
- **Universal sparse/contextual dictionary:** rejected because similar high-resolution exemplars do not exist for all regimes and local patch matching does not guarantee national coherence.
- **Universal procedural/process library:** rejected because partial causal models become generic functions outside calibrated domains and explode at physical transitions.
- **Generic geostatistical roughness:** rejected because matching spectra or variograms alone produces noise rather than recognizable material/process morphology.
- **Orthophoto displacement or optical height supervision:** rejected because albedo, objects, vegetation, water, shadow, and scale do not equal ground geometry.
- **Two x4 networks mandated by LOD layout:** rejected; model factorization is selected by target evidence and bake-off, while packed parents are always derived from the canonical finest surface.
- **Independent per-chunk generation or seeding:** rejected because chunk identity would control phase, class, and features and would reproduce visible seams/pattern changes.
- **Unsupported-regime fallback:** rejected. A generic or nearest-class filler would falsely satisfy “detail everywhere” while lowering the quality ceiling.
- **Runtime procedural displacement:** rejected because it violates the zero-browser-synthesis law.
- **Claiming vertical/undercut cliffs through a heightfield:** rejected as topologically impossible.

## 10. Cheapest Decisive Proof

Do not build the national model first. Run one parent-closed research proof with three independently scored tracks.

### Track A: evidence and target qualification

Fully qualify the Moore peat surfaces and at least one converted raw candidate, preserving effective resolution, error, visible-ground semantics, interpolation/support, site identity, conditions, license, and hashes. In parallel, define the minimum Estonia acquisition needed for the first production regime. Estimate signal-to-measurement-error separately for the 1-0.25 m and 0.25-0.0625 m ranges. If the finest signal is not observable, Stage-B claims stop.

### Track B: typed Taevaskoda source repair

Over the existing Taevaskoda parent and its full containing corrected LOD 0/support domain, reconstruct water surface/bed distinction, smooth world-space shoreline, bank side/shoulder, mapped shoreline escarpment, and diagnosed bumps from DTM plus ETAK and dated imagery. Compare deterministic reconstruction with calibrated learned assistance only on ambiguous cells. Publish corrected ordinary LOD 0 and derive the full fine/ancestor closure. This track proves removal of the visible 1 m grid and source errors; it does not claim the non-heightfield cliff wall.

### Track C: one-regime morphology bake-off

Use peat first because it is the only currently ready target-scale height family, while retaining its foreign/unpaired limitations. Compare contextual exemplar, measured conditional simulation, event/process hypotheses supported by the peat evidence, learned regressor, GAN, pixel-space diffusion, and justified hybrids through the same finest-surface and packed hierarchy adapter. Hold out complete sites and judge blind ground-level renders, morphology distribution coverage, memorization/repetition, partition invariance, packed entropy, and cook cost.

The proof passes only if:

- Track B visibly removes the stepped shore and water artifacts without destroying the real bank/escarpment or creating LOD motion;
- one Track C specialist clearly beats all fair challengers for the supported peat scope without becoming generic roughness or copying a source plot;
- the exact parent-closed Estonia URL boots cleanly and shows terrain, materials, trees/plants, grass, and water on the same packed surface;
- the result is represented honestly as one proven specialist, not evidence that peat architecture generalizes nationally.

This is the cheapest proof that can falsify the architectural risks while exercising the accepted cook/packing infrastructure. A national diffusion training run, broad uncalibrated process catalog, or multi-terabyte cook before these gates would spend more while answering less.

## 11. Primary Evidence Boundaries Used

- [Guérin et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1111/cgf.12821): paired sparse exemplar amplification; similar exemplars required; no guaranteed geomorphological or small-scale coherence.
- [Argudo et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00371-017-1393-6): coherent multi-layer contextual exemplar synthesis; not the orthophoto FCN and not centimeter national transfer.
- [Argudo et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1111/cgf.13345): orthophoto-guided 15 m-to-2 m terrain inference with vegetation and shadow failures.
- [Zhao et al. 2019, GATA](https://doi.org/10.1145/3355089.3356553): learned x4 multi-theme amplification; no reusable Estonia target data or checkpoint.
- [Bar-Tal et al. 2023, MultiDiffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08113): shared-state overlap consensus for image crops; no terrain derivative, drainage, or prior guarantee.
- [Wang et al. 2023, DDNM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.00490): range/null consistency for an explicit linear observation; no real Maa-amet degradation or terrain prior.
- [Schott et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1145/3658200): strong multiscale hydraulic amplification in its valid family; whole-domain and non-hydraulic limitations remain.
- [Doane et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1029/2024AV001264): event production/decay and ideal pit/mound forms; not calibrated Estonia event distributions.
- [Moore et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.5194/bg-16-3491-2019): measured 1 cm peat surfaces and scale relations in small prepared foreign plots; not national context or transfer proof.
- [Pawlik et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geomorph.2024.109283): 0.025 m TLS evidence that 1 m ALS misses forest pit-mound morphology at one site; no national parameters or open target corpus.
- [Paris et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1145/3342765): implicit 3D features demonstrate why overhangs need non-heightfield representation; not a frozen-heightfield centimeter solution.
- [Dhaouadi et al. 2026, GrounDiff](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/html/Dhaouadi_GrounDiff_Diffusion-Based_Ground_Surface_Generation_from_Digital_Surface_Models_WACV_2026_paper.html): learned DSM-to-DTM correction with cliff and dense-vegetation failures; not unresolved morphology synthesis.

The detailed paper, repository, target-data, Estonia-input, regime, and cook-contract audits remain the evidence ledger for the replacement specification. This dossier arbitrates them; it does not broaden any source beyond what it directly demonstrates.
